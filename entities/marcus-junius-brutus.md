# Marcus Junius Brutus

Cited in Seneca's *Consolation to Helvia* as the author of a treatise *On Virtue*, in which he recorded visiting the exiled Marcellus at Mytilene and feeling that he, Brutus, was the one departing into exile by leaving his admired friend behind. Separately, after Caesar's assassination, Brutus attended a tense family council at his mother Servilia's villa at Antium, alongside [[entities/gaius-cassius-longinus|Cassius]] and Cicero, to debate the conspirators' political strategy. This page covers these two specific documented episodes; Brutus's central role in planning and carrying out Caesar's assassination itself is covered elsewhere in the wiki.
