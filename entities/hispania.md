# Hispania

The Iberian peninsula was organized into the Roman provinces of Hispania Citerior ("Nearer Spain") and Hispania Ulterior ("Further Spain") following the Second Punic War. [[entities/cato-the-elder]] invoked his own military record there during a Spanish campaign as a favorable contrast in his prosecution of [[entities/manius-acilius-glabrio]]. In the 70s BC it became the base for [[entities/quintus-sertorius]]'s breakaway Roman regime, which held out against the Sullan government until his assassination.
