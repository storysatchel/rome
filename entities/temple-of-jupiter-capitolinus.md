# Temple of Jupiter Capitolinus

The Temple of Jupiter Capitolinus (Jupiter Optimus Maximus) stood atop the [[entities/capitoline-hill]] as Rome's principal state cult site. Rebuilt after a Sullan-era fire, its restoration was overseen by [[entities/quintus-lutatius-catulus]], but stalled progress on the project gave [[entities/julius-caesar]] a pretext, as praetor in 62 BC, to publicly summon and humiliate Catulus over it.
