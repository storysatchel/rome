# Sergestus

Trojan captain of the Centaur in the Book V boat race, identified by Virgil as the legendary ancestor of the Sergian house (gens Sergia). In his zeal to cut the turn too close, he wrecks his ship on an offshore reef and limps home last, oars broken, but is still rewarded by Aeneas for bringing his crew home safely.
