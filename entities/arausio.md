# Arausio

Arausio (modern Orange) was the site in [[entities/gaul]] of a catastrophic Roman defeat in October 105 BC, when an entire army was destroyed by the invading Cimbri. [[entities/quintus-servilius-caepio]]'s refusal to cooperate with his co-commander Gnaeus Mallius Maximus was blamed for the disaster, fueling both his subsequent prosecution over the Tolosan gold and the later animosity between his son and Marcus Aemilius Scaurus.
