# Spurius Carvilius Ruga

Spurius Carvilius, surnamed Ruga, divorced his wife on grounds of infertility in a case ancient jurists dated variously between 234 and 227 BCE. Because his wife had committed no fault, the case was remembered (per the jurist Servius Sulpicius, via Aulus Gellius) as forcing the formalization of dowry-restitution guarantees in Roman divorce law.
