# Cannae

Cannae, in southern [[entities/italy]], was the site of Rome's worst single military defeat of the Second Punic War, inflicted by Hannibal in 216 BC. The ensuing civic panic at [[entities/rome]] formed the backdrop to the same-year *incestum* conviction of the Vestals [[entities/opimia-vestal-216-bc]] and [[entities/floronia-vestal-216-bc]].
