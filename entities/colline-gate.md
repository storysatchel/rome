# Colline Gate

The Colline Gate was a gate in [[entities/rome]]'s city wall, near which stood the [[entities/campus-sceleratus]] ("field of wickedness"), the underground execution site reserved for [[entities/vestal-virgins]] convicted of *incestum*. Vestals including [[entities/minucia-vestal-337-bc]] and [[entities/opimia-vestal-216-bc]] were buried alive there.
