# Entellus

An elderly Sicilian boxer, once trained by the divine boxer Eryx, who is goaded out of retirement by his patron Acestes to fight the young champion Dares. After falling to one of Dares's dodges, Entellus returns with fury and beats Dares nearly to death before Aeneas stops the bout; he then kills the prize bull with one blow of his gauntlet and retires from boxing for good.
