# Clodius Albinus

Roman governor of Britain, proclaimed emperor by his legions during the civil wars following the "Year of the Five Emperors" (AD 193). Initially recognized by Septimius Severus as a junior colleague, their alliance broke down and Albinus crossed into Gaul, gaining support from Gallic centers including Lugdunum, before being decisively defeated and killed by Severus at the Battle of Lyon in AD 197.
