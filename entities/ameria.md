# Ameria

Ameria was a town in [[entities/umbria]], home to the family of [[entities/sextus-roscius]], whose father was murdered in 80 BC in a scheme to have his Ameria estate declared proscribed property and sold cheaply to associates of Sulla's freedman Chrysogonus.^[inferred]
