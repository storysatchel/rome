# Eurytion

Brother of Pandarus (the archer who broke the Trojan-Greek truce at Troy), Eurytion is the only contestant actually to kill the tethered dove in the Book V archery contest. Aeneas nonetheless awards first prize to Acestes on the strength of a fiery omen; Eurytion accepts the ruling without complaint and takes second place.
