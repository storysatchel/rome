# C. Titinius (of Minturnae)

A man from the Latin town of Minturnae who divorced his wife Fannia around 100 BC and attempted to retain a portion of her dowry on the grounds that her own conduct had caused the divorce (*retentio propter mores*). He lost the resulting suit, with the juror C. Marius ordering him to repay almost the full dowry.
