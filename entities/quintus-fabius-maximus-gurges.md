# Quintus Fabius Maximus Gurges

Son of the distinguished general Quintus Fabius Rullianus, nicknamed "Gurges" ("the Glutton" or "Whirlpool") for squandering his paternal inheritance. As a junior magistrate (likely aedile) he fined a group of married Roman matrons for adultery. As consul in 292 BC he suffered a defeat against the Samnites and faced removal from his army by the Senate, but his father successfully pleaded to serve as his legate instead, and the pair went on to defeat the Samnites and triumph, beheading the enemy commander Gaius Pontius. He later clashed with interrex-turned-consul Lucius Postumius Megellus over his provincial command in Samnium in 291 BC, and was elected consul twice more.
