# Tolosa

Tolosa (modern Toulouse) was a sanctuary city in [[entities/gaul]] holding a vast sacred hoard of gold and silver. As proconsul, [[entities/quintus-servilius-caepio]] seized this treasure, and was widely — if never formally — believed to have embezzled much of the "Tolosan gold" for himself, an affair that became proverbial for bringing ruin on whoever touched it.
