# Turnus

King of the Rutulians, betrothed (by his own claim) to Lavinia, and the chief human antagonist of the Aeneid's second half. In Book X he kills the young Arcadian prince Pallas in single combat and takes his swordbelt as a trophy; in Book XII, forced into single combat with Aeneas by public pressure, he is wounded and begs for mercy, but Aeneas — recognizing Pallas's belt on Turnus's shoulder — kills him instead.
