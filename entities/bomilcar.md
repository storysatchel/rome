# Bomilcar

Jugurtha's most trusted servant, Bomilcar organized the hired assassination of the Numidian prince Massiva in Rome in 112 BC, using bribed spies to track the victim's movements. Formally charged with the murder and smuggled out of Rome by Jugurtha to avoid trial, Bomilcar later plotted against his own king during the Jugurthine War and was put to death for it.
