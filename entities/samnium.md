# Samnium

Samnium was the rugged, mountainous region of south-central Italy inhabited by the Samnites, a confederation of hill peoples against whom Rome fought three major wars between 343 and 290 BC. Much of the clan Fabius's early military record — including several command disputes and the humiliation at the Caudine Forks — is set here.
