# Gaius Pontius

Samnite general who, during the Second Samnite War, trapped a Roman consular army in the mountain pass known as the Caudine Forks (321 BC), forcing its surrender and the humiliating ritual of passing "under the yoke" before negotiating a treaty with Rome.
