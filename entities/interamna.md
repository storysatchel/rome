# Interamna (Nahars)

Interamna (modern Terni), an Umbrian town sited below the falls created by [[entities/manius-curius-dentatus]]'s canal draining Lake Velinus, complained for roughly two centuries that the canal endangered its situation, ultimately bringing the dispute before a Roman senatorial commission in 54 BCE against [[entities/reate]].
