# Britannia

Britannia was the Roman province governed in the 190s AD by [[entities/clodius-albinus]], who commanded its legions and was proclaimed emperor by his troops before crossing to [[entities/gaul]] to challenge [[entities/septimius-severus]], a conflict that ended at the Battle of [[entities/lugdunum]] in AD 197.
