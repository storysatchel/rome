# L. Sergius Catilina (pr. 68 BC)

Praetor in 68 BC and governor of Africa (67–66 BC), twice acquitted in legal proceedings this page covers before his notorious 63 BC conspiracy: first of extorting Africa in 65 BC, in a trial whose prosecutor, P. Clodius Pulcher, Cicero suspected of deliberate collusion; and again in 64 BC, of killing proscribed Romans during Sulla's dictatorship, a charge for which two more obscure defendants (Bellienus and Luscius) were convicted in the same court around the same time. In 73 BC he was also implicated, though apparently not formally tried, in the Vestal Fabia's incestum accusation.
