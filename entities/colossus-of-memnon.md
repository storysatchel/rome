# Colossus of Memnon

A massive seated statue of Amenhotep III at [[entities/thebes-egypt]], cracked by an earthquake so that it emitted a strange sound at dawn, popularly believed to be the hero Memnon greeting his mother Eos. Visitors across the empire left inscriptions recording whether they "heard Memnon"; when [[entities/hadrian]] visited in AD 130, it reportedly sounded for his wife [[entities/vibia-sabina]] but stayed silent for him.
