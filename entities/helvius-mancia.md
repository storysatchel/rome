# Helvius Mancia

A son of a freedman, Helvius Mancia had denounced L. Scribonius Libo to the censors. At Libo's trial in 55 BC, Pompey tried to denigrate Mancia for his low birth; Mancia responded by publicly cataloguing the killings Pompey had carried out decades earlier as a young enforcer in Sulla's service during the civil wars of the 80s BC, embarrassing the great general in open court.
