# Ascanius

Aeneas's son, also known as Iulus (legendary ancestor of the Julian line). In Book IX, with Aeneas away, Ascanius heads the besieged Trojan camp's council and grants Nisus and Euryalus permission for their night raid, promising lavish rewards for their mission to reach Aeneas.
