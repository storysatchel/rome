# Red Sea

The Red Sea route linked Ptolemaic Egypt's ports to [[entities/india]] and Arabia. Indian and Arab merchants had long guarded knowledge of the monsoon winds that made the open-sea crossing possible; [[entities/eudoxus-of-cyzicus]] used a rescued Indian guide's knowledge to launch two pioneering Red Sea–to–India voyages from here around 120 BC.
