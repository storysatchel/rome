# M. Tullius (plaintiff, 71 BC, not Cicero)

A landowner near Thurii — no relation to the orator M. Tullius Cicero, despite the identical name — who sued his neighbor P. Fabius in 71 BC after Fabius's men allegedly attacked his slaves and property with weapons. Represented by Cicero in the fragmentary surviving speech *Pro Tullio*.
