# Quintus Lutatius Catulus (cos. 78 BC)

A senior senator and consul of 78 BC, Catulus was the leading candidate for pontifex maximus in 63 BC but lost the election, controversially, to the much younger Julius Caesar. He had accused Caesar of complicity with the Catilinarian conspirators, and as praetor in 62 BC Caesar took public revenge by summoning Catulus to a contio and demanding he account for the stalled restoration of the Temple of Jupiter Capitolinus, reportedly forcing him to speak from the ground rather than the rostra.
