# L. Quinctius (tr. pl. 74 BC)

Tribune of the plebs in 74 BC, later praetor in 68 BC. As tribune he drove several of the follow-on corruption prosecutions against jurors and the presiding magistrate implicated in the *iudicium Iunianum* bribery scandal. He also appeared as a civil advocate, defending P. Fabius in the 71 BC Fabius–Tullius land dispute (Cicero's *Pro Tullio*).
