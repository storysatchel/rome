# M. Antonius (orator, cos. 99 BC)

Consul in 99 BC, censor in 97 BC, and one of the two leading orators of his generation (alongside L. Licinius Crassus), celebrated in Cicero's *de Oratore* and *Brutus*. He was the paternal grandfather of the triumvir Mark Antony — a distinct historical figure from [[entities/mark-antony]]. He was implicated in the 113 BC special quaestio investigating the Vestal scandal and was acquitted after speaking in his own defense, having waived a procedural right to decline the charge while traveling on public business.
