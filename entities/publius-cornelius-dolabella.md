# Publius Cornelius Dolabella

A young Caesarian noble elected to a planned consulship for 44 BC alongside Mark Antony. Antony had divorced his wife Antonia on grounds of her adultery with Dolabella and used his religious office as augur to try to invalidate Dolabella's election out of personal grudge, before the two were reconciled after Caesar's assassination. Not to be confused with Cn. Cornelius Dolabella (cos. 81 BC), prosecuted decades earlier by the young Caesar.
