# Lucius Furius (defendant, De Aqua)

Target of Cato's censorial speech *In L. Furium de aqua* ("Against Lucius Furius, concerning the water"). Cato alleged Furius had purchased land at a high price in the expectation of irrigating it with water illegally diverted from the public supply; some scholars tentatively identify him with L. Furius Purpurio, consul of 196 BC, though this is uncertain.
