# C. Porcius Cato (cos. 114 BC)

Consul in 114 BC and grandson of [[entities/cato-the-elder]] — a distinct person from his famous grandfather. He was convicted of extortion in Macedonia in 113 BC (fined only a token 8,000 sesterces), and convicted again in 109 BC by the special Mamilian Commission for treasonous collusion with Jugurtha, going into exile at Tarraco.
