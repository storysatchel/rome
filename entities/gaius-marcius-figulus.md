# C. Marcius Figulus (cos. 162, 156 BC)

Consul in 162 BC (and again in 156 BC). He was forced to abdicate his first consulship within the year after Ti. Sempronius Gracchus, the consul who had presided over the election, belatedly reported a ritual error in its conduct to the College of Augurs.
