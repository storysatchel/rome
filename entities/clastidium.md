# Clastidium

Clastidium was a town in [[entities/cisalpine-gaul]], near which the consul [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus]] met the Insubrian king [[entities/viridomarus]] in single combat in 222 BC, killing him and winning the *spolia opima*. During this campaign Marcellus vowed the temple to Honos he dedicated in Rome, which he later tried and failed to jointly rededicate to Virtus in 208 BC.
