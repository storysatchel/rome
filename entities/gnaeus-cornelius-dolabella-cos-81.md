# Cn. Cornelius Dolabella (cos. 81 BC)

Consul in 81 BC, a favorite of the Senate, who governed Macedonia from 80 to 77 BC. On his return he was prosecuted for extortion by a 23-year-old [[entities/julius-caesar]] in Caesar's first major recorded courtroom appearance. Defended by the advocates C. Aurelius Cotta and Q. Hortensius Hortalus, Dolabella secured the best available legal talent and was acquitted.
