# Langenses Viturii

The Langenses Viturii were a local Ligurian community whose fields bordered the territory of the allied city of [[entities/genua]]. A boundary dispute between the two was resolved in 117 BC by two Roman senatorial legates, whose verdict (the *sententia Minuciorum*) was inscribed on the disputed ground.
