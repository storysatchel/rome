# Sergius and Matienus (Military Tribunes at Locri)

Two military tribunes commanding an independent detachment of soldiers at occupied Locri, separate from Gaius Pleminius's garrison, in 205 BC. When a soldier under Pleminius's command stole a silver cup from a Locrian, Sergius and Matienus intervened to return it; the ensuing dispute escalated into a street brawl, after which Pleminius had the two tribunes seized, stripped, and prepared for a beating — provoking their soldiers to retaliate by mutilating Pleminius. Scipio Africanus later shackled the two tribunes and sent them to Rome to be judged by the Senate.
