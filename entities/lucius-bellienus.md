# L. Bellienus (pr. 105 BC)

Praetor in 105 BC. In 64 BC, nearly two decades after the fact, he was convicted under the *lex Cornelia de sicariis et veneficis* of killing Q. Lucretius Afella — a Sullan partisan killed for defying Sulla's own authority — before a court presided over by the young Julius Caesar.
