# Sex. Aebutius

Defendant accused by A. Caecina of using an armed gang to forcibly prevent Caecina from taking possession of disputed land near Volaterrae, in a civil suit (c. 69/68 BC) argued by Cicero for the plaintiff and defended by C. Calpurnius Piso. The surviving evidence suggests Caecina ultimately prevailed.
