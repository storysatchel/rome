# The Trojan Camp (Siege of Book IX)

The fortified camp the Trojans build on landing in [[entities/latium]], besieged by [[entities/turnus]]'s Rutulian army in Book IX of the *Aeneid* while [[entities/aeneas]] is away seeking allies. Command of the camp in his absence falls to his son [[entities/ascanius]]. It is from here that [[entities/nisus]] and [[entities/euryalus]] set out on their night raid through the besiegers' lines.
