# Gnaeus Flavius

A man of obscure, low-born origin — his father had been a freed slave — elected curule aedile in the wake of Appius Claudius's controversial 312 BC censorship, which had reassigned voters (including a rising "forum faction") across the voting tribes. As aedile, Flavius broke aristocratic and priestly monopolies by publicizing the previously hidden rules and procedures for civil lawsuits (long guarded by the pontiffs) and publicly displaying the religious calendar.
