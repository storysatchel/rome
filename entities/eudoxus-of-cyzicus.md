# Eudoxus of Cyzicus

A Greek navigator active around 120-108 BC, working under the patronage of King Ptolemy VIII of Egypt. He made two pioneering open-sea voyages from the Red Sea to India using the knowledge of a rescued shipwrecked Indian guide, but had his cargo confiscated by Ptolemaic customs both times. He then attempted to reach India by circumnavigating Africa, disappearing without trace during his second attempt.
