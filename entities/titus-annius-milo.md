# Titus Annius Milo

A tribune and gang leader allied with the nobility, Milo organized armed bands to oppose Clodius's followers, helping force the recall of Cicero from exile in 57 BC. His running street war with Clodius ended when the two met by chance on the Appian Way near Bovillae in January 52 BC and Clodius was killed. Milo was prosecuted for the murder, defended unsuccessfully by Cicero, and went into exile at Massilia.
