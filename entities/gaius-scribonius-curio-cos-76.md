# C. Scribonius Curio (cos. 76 BC)

Consul in 76 BC and censor in 61 BC. A recurring figure in the legal life of his generation: he co-prosecuted the defendant Titinia around 79 BC (reportedly forgetting his speech and blaming it on her magic), and in other years appears as a repetundae defendant over his governorship of Macedonia and as a party to ambitus proceedings.
