# Temple of Amphiaraus (Oropus)

The temple of Amphiaraus stood at [[entities/oropus]] in [[entities/boeotia]], a sanctuary to the healing hero-god Amphiaraus. During his Greek campaigns in the 80s BC, [[entities/sulla]] vowed and granted the temple's surrounding land inviolable, tax-exempt status. When Roman publicani later assessed dues on that same land, Oropus's successful petition to Rome in 74-73 BC preserved the sanctuary's exemption.
