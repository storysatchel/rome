# G. Fidiculanius Falcula

A senator who served as one of the thirty-two jurors in the 74 BC *iudicium Iunianum* that convicted Oppianicus, and was accused by Cicero of having accepted bribes to vote for conviction. He was separately prosecuted twice in the scandal's aftermath (in 74 BC under the *lex Cornelia de repetundis*, outcome uncertain, and again in 73 BC, when he was acquitted).
