# Temple of Concordia

The Temple of Concordia stood in the [[entities/forum-romanum]]. [[entities/lucius-opimius]] built it after his acquittal for the killings carried out in suppressing [[entities/gaius-gracchus]], ostensibly to commemorate restored civic concord — provoking a bitter graffito suggesting the "doctor" of civil strife might have been madder than the "patient."
