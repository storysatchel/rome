# The Sea-Rock Turning Marker (Drepanum Boat Race)

A rocky outcrop in the sea off [[entities/drepanum]], chosen by [[entities/aeneas]] as the outbound turning point for the four-ship race of [[scenarios/aeneid-funeral-games-boat-race]]. It is at this marker that [[entities/gyas]] orders his helmsman to hug the shoreline, [[entities/cloanthus]] slips inside to take the lead, and [[entities/sergestus]] wrecks the *Centaur* on a nearby reef while cutting the turn too aggressively.
