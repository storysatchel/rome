# Genua

Genua was an allied coastal city in Liguria, on the site of modern Genoa. In 117 BC it was party to a boundary dispute with the inland Ligurian community of the Langenses Viturii, which the senate resolved by dispatching two legates to arbitrate and inscribe a settlement (the *sententia Minuciorum*).
