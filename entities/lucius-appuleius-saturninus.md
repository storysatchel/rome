# Lucius Appuleius Saturninus

Populist tribune of the plebs, elected an unprecedented third time for 99 BC alongside his ally Gaius Servilius Glaucia's consular bid. When fellow candidate Gaius Memmius threatened to block Glaucia's election, hired agents of Saturninus and Glaucia beat Memmius to death during the voting. The Senate declared Saturninus and Glaucia public enemies; Marius, called on to suppress his former political allies, besieged them on the Capitol, and Saturninus was stoned to death by a mob despite assurances of safety. His tribunate also forced [[entities/quintus-caecilius-metellus-numidicus|Metellus Numidicus]] into exile over a loyalty-oath dispute, and his political ally [[entities/quintus-servilius-caepio|Caepio]] was prosecuted in the fallout of the Tolosan gold scandal that Saturninus helped engineer.
