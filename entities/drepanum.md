# Drepanum

Drepanum, on the western coast of Sicily, is where [[entities/aeneas]]'s father Anchises dies in the *Aeneid*'s narrative, and where Aeneas's Sicilian host [[entities/acestes]] rules. On the first anniversary of Anchises's death, Aeneas holds the funeral games of Book V there — foot race, boat race, boxing match, and archery contest — before continuing on to Italy. ^[inferred: Virgil's text does not name "Drepanum" explicitly within the individual games scenarios, but Book V's narrative frame places the games at this Sicilian harbor associated with Acestes and Anchises's death]
