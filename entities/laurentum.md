# Laurentum

Laurentum is the legendary city and territory of King Latinus in the *Aeneid*, whose throne and whose daughter Lavinia's hand are the stakes of the poem's second-half war between the Trojans (led by [[entities/aeneas]]) and the Rutulians (led by [[entities/turnus]]). The pitched battles of Books IX–XII, including the deaths of [[entities/pallas]] and Turnus, unfold on Laurentine ground. ^[inferred: individual scenario pages drawn from these books generally say "Latium" rather than naming "Laurentum" specifically, but the legendary capital city of King Latinus is the conventional name for this district within the Aeneid's geography]
