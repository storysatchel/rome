# Valeria Messalina

Third wife of the emperor Claudius. According to Suetonius, she rode in a carpentum following Claudius's chariot during his triumph celebrating the conquest of Britain in AD 44, an unusual public share of triumphal honor normally reserved for the triumphator alone.
