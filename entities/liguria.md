# Liguria

Liguria was the coastal and mountainous region of northwestern Italy inhabited by the Ligurians, bordering Cisalpine Gaul. It was home to the allied city of [[entities/genua]] (modern Genoa) and the inland community of the Langenses Viturii, whose 117 BC boundary dispute Roman legates arbitrated in the *sententia Minuciorum*.
