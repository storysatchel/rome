# Thebes (Egypt)

Thebes was an Upper Egyptian city on the Nile, site of the [[entities/colossus-of-memnon]], one of the ancient world's premier tourist attractions. The emperor [[entities/hadrian]] visited with his wife [[entities/vibia-sabina]] and the poetess [[entities/julia-balbilla]] in AD 130.
