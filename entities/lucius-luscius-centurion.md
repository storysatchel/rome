# L. Luscius (centurion)

A former centurion who, nearly two decades after Sulla's proscriptions, was convicted in 64 BC under the *lex Cornelia de sicariis et veneficis* of having killed three men formally placed on Sulla's proscription lists. The court was presided over by the young Julius Caesar.
