# Hippocoon

Son of Hyrtacus (and so a kinsman of Nisus), Hippocoon draws the first shot in the Book V archery contest; his arrow strikes and sticks fast in the mast that holds the target dove, a creditable but non-winning shot that leaves him with the contest's consolation prize.
