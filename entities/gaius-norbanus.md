# Gaius Norbanus

A tribune of the people who, together with Lucius Appuleius Saturninus, proposed the extraordinary judicial commission that prosecuted Quintus Servilius Caepio for embezzlement and treason connected to the defeat at Arausio. Norbanus was later himself brought to trial for having set the proceedings in motion.
