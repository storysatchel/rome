# Titinia (defendant, c. 79 BC)

Defendant in a private civil suit (*iudicium privatum*) around 79 BC whose underlying claim is unrecorded, but which became memorable because the two prosecutors, Ser. Naevius and C. Scribonius Curio, forgot their prepared speeches mid-trial and publicly attributed the lapse to a spell Titinia had supposedly cast on them. Defended by C. Aurelius Cotta and a young M. Tullius Cicero.
