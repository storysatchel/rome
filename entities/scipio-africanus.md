# Publius Cornelius Scipio Africanus

Roman general who conquered Spain from Carthage as a young proconsul and, as consul of 205 BC, pushed for and eventually won Senate approval to invade Africa against Fabius Maximus's objections. Not to be confused with his grandson by adoption, Scipio Aemilianus. His legate Gaius Pleminius's brutal misconduct at Locri briefly threatened to derail his command; during the same African campaign a dispute arose over quaestorial funds involving [[entities/cato-the-elder|Cato the Elder]]. Late in life, politically marginalized, he withdrew in a self-imposed exile to his villa at Liternum rather than answer further Senate attacks.
