# Massilia

Massilia (modern Marseille) was a Greek-founded port city on the Gallic coast within the Roman sphere of [[entities/gaul]]. [[entities/titus-annius-milo]] went into exile here after his conviction for the killing of [[entities/clodius-pulcher]].
