# Flavius (tribune of the plebs, 60 BC)

Flavius, tribune of the plebs in 60 BC, promoted an agrarian bill to settle [[entities/pompey]]'s discharged veterans on land. When consul [[entities/metellus-celer]] opposed the measure, Flavius threatened to block him from his province and had him briefly imprisoned — an escalation that nonetheless failed to secure the bill's passage.
