# Quintus Caecilius Metellus Numidicus

Roman consul of 109 BC who commanded Roman forces against Jugurtha in Numidia. His legate Gaius Marius campaigned for and won the command away from him, igniting a fierce rivalry between the two men that fed into the wider political realignments of the late Republic. A decade later, as a senator, he was the sole member of the Senate to refuse, on principle, to swear the oath required by Saturninus's 100 BC land law. His refusal led to his forcible removal from the Senate house and eventual exile; he was recalled in 99 BC amid great popular support, and a tribune who had tried to block his recall was later murdered by a mob.
