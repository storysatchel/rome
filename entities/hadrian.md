# Hadrian

Roman emperor (AD 117-138), noted for extensive personal travels throughout the empire. During a visit to Egypt in AD 130 with his wife Vibia Sabina and an entourage including the poetess Julia Balbilla, he visited the famous "singing" Colossus of Memnon at Thebes; surviving graffiti suggests the statue failed to perform for him on this occasion, while it reportedly spoke for Sabina.
