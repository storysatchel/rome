# Publicius Malleolus

Convicted by the Roman people (*iudicium populi*) of killing his own mother in 101 BC. He was executed by the archaic *poena cullei*, sewn into a leather sack and thrown into the sea — one of the few historically (rather than legendarily) attested Republican applications of this punishment. No other biographical details survive.
