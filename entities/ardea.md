# Ardea

A Latin coastal city near Rome. In 446 BC, Ardea and neighboring Aricia were unable to resolve a boundary dispute between themselves and jointly submitted it to Roman arbitration. Rome's failure to decide the case resulted in Rome annexing the disputed land as its own public property.
