# Caesar's Cremation Site (Roman Forum)

After [[entities/julius-caesar]]'s assassination in March 44 BC, his body was cremated at a specific spot within the [[entities/forum-romanum]]. In the volatile mourning that followed, [[entities/amatius-false-marius]] attempted to dedicate an altar on this exact site, seeking to claim both Marius's and Caesar's popular legacies at once.^[inferred]
