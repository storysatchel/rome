# M. Aurelius Scaurus (cos. suff. 108 BC)

Suffect consul of 108 BC, possibly one of three *quaesitores* presiding over the special Mamilian Commission's 109 BC treason trials against commanders and officials accused of colluding with King Jugurtha. The identity of the quaesitor is disputed: ancient testimony names the more famous M. Aemilius Scaurus (cos. 115 BC), while one reconstruction identifies him instead as this Aurelius Scaurus.
