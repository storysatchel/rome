# Domitius (consular candidate, 54 BC)

Domitius — probably Cn. Domitius Calvinus — was a candidate for the consulship of 53 BC alongside [[entities/gaius-memmius-cos-candidate54]]. The two secretly contracted with outgoing consul [[entities/appius-claudius-pulcher]] to produce false augural witnesses to a lex curiata once elected, or forfeit a sum of money.
