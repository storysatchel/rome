# River Nar

The river Nar flowed through [[entities/umbria]] past [[entities/interamna]]; [[entities/manius-curius-dentatus]]'s canal emptied Lake Velinus into it in 272 BCE, creating the falls at what is now the Cascate delle Marmore and the waters that Interamna blamed for its flooding in the [[scenarios/reate-interamna-velinus-lake-dispute|Reate–Interamna dispute]].
