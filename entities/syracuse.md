# Syracuse

Syracuse was the great Greek city of [[entities/sicilia]] that [[entities/scipio-africanus]] used as his staging base while preparing his invasion of Africa. From Syracuse he sailed to [[entities/locri]] to investigate the garrison atrocities committed by his legate [[entities/gaius-pleminius]].
