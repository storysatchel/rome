# Marcus Claudius Marcellus (consul 51 BCE)

A Pompeian-aligned consul ^[inferred — not explicitly dated in Seneca's text] who, per Seneca's *Consolation to Helvia*, withdrew into voluntary exile at Mytilene after the civil war turned against his faction, living a life of philosophical study that Brutus praised as happier than his former consulship. Distinct from the earlier Republican general [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus]], conqueror of Syracuse (211 BC).
