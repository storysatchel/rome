# C. Aurelius Cotta (cos. 75 BC)

Consul in 75 BC and a prominent advocate of his generation. He defended Titinia around 79 BC alongside a young Cicero (Cicero's second recorded courtroom speech), and separately joined Q. Hortensius Hortalus in defending Cn. Cornelius Dolabella against the young Julius Caesar's 77 BC extortion prosecution.
