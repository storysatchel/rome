# Lucius Valerius Flaccus

Patrician senator, consul in 195 BC alongside Cato the Elder, and his colleague in the censorship of 184 BC. The two acted in full harmony as censors — reviewing the Senate, expelling seven members, and auctioning public contracts at terms that provoked a backlash from the contractors and Titus Flamininus.
