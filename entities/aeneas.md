# Aeneas

Trojan prince and son of Venus, Virgil's protagonist, who leads the Trojan survivors to Italy and fights to found a Trojan foothold in Latium — the legendary ancestor of the Roman people. Beyond the broad narrative already covered on [[references/virgil-the-aeneid]], Aeneas appears as host/judge of Anchises's funeral games in Sicily and as the victor of the war's climactic single combat.
