# Via Appia (Appian Way)

The Via Appia was Rome's great southern road, running out of the city through [[entities/latium]] past [[entities/bovillae]] toward Campania. The retinues of [[entities/clodius-pulcher]] and [[entities/titus-annius-milo]] encountered each other on this road near Bovillae on 20 January 52 BC, in the clash that left Clodius dead.
