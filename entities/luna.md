# Luna

Luna was a Roman colony on the border of [[entities/etruria]] and [[entities/liguria]]. In 168 BC it disputed boundaries with the neighboring town of [[entities/pisae]], one of the arbitrated Italian land disputes Gargola cites alongside the *sententia Minuciorum* between [[entities/genua]] and the Langenses Viturii.
