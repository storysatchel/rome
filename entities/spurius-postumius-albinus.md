# Sp. Postumius Albinus (cos. 110 BC)

Consul in 110 BC and proconsul in Africa and Numidia in 109 BC during the opening phase of the Jugurthine War. Convicted that same year by the special Mamilian Commission investigating bribery and collusion with King Jugurtha.
