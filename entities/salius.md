# Salius

An Acarnanian who holds second place in the Book V foot race until the falling Nisus deliberately trips him to clear the way for Euryalus. Salius protests the foul loudly before the assembled "front-row elders," and Aeneas, while refusing to change the official order, consoles him with a lion's hide.
