# Lucius Postumius Megellus

Roman statesman who served as interrex in 291 BC and was elected consul in the very assembly he himself presided over — a clear conflict of interest that the Senate nonetheless let stand. As consul he wrote to proconsul Fabius Gurges in Samnium demanding he evacuate the province so Postumius could rightfully command there himself, provoking a senatorial rebuke.
