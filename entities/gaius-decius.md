# Gaius Decius (prosecutor of Opimius)

Known chiefly through a fragment preserved by Cicero, Decius was the prosecutor in the trial of Lucius Opimius, where he put the case's central constitutional question bluntly: "You could not lawfully kill even the most wicked citizen without a trial" (*Ne sceleratissimum quidem civem sine judicio jure ullo necare potuisti*).
