# A. Postumius Albinus (pr. by 111? BC)

A praetor (by 111 BC) and legate in 110 BC, implicated in a disastrous and possibly bribery-tainted treaty his brother Sp. Postumius Albinus's army made with Jugurtha. Possibly identical to the later A. Postumius Albinus, cos. 99 BC. Ancient sources disagree on whether he was ever formally tried by the 109 BC Mamilian Commission, or acquitted if he was.
