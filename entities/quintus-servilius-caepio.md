# Quintus Servilius Caepio (consul 106 BC)

As consul in 106 BC, Caepio tried to wrest control of the extortion-court juries back from the equites. As proconsul he looted the sacred treasure of Tolosa in Gaul, and his refusal to cooperate with his co-commander Gnaeus Mallius Maximus contributed directly to Rome's catastrophic defeat at Arausio in 105 BC. He was stripped of his command, expelled from the Senate, and ultimately condemned by a special tribunician commission investigating both the Tolosan embezzlement and the Arausio disaster.
