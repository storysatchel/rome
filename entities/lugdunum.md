# Lugdunum (Lyon)

A Roman colonia founded in the mid-to-late 40s BC in Gallia Lugdunensis, which went on to serve as the province's capital and one of the pivotal cities of the Gallo-Roman urban network. It backed the usurper Clodius Albinus in the civil war against Septimius Severus, and was the site of the decisive battle (AD 197) in which Severus defeated and killed Albinus; the city was subsequently penalized for its support of the losing side.
