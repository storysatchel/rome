# Rostra

The Rostra was the raised speaking platform in the [[entities/forum-romanum]] from which magistrates addressed the *contio* and conducted much of Rome's public oratory. Its elevation carried symbolic weight: when [[entities/julius-caesar]], as praetor in 62 BC, reportedly demanded that [[entities/quintus-lutatius-catulus]] answer for the stalled Capitoline temple restoration while standing on the ground rather than the Rostra, it was understood as a calculated act of public humiliation.
