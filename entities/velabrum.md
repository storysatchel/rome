# Velabrum

The Velabrum was a low, once-marshy district of [[entities/rome]] near the [[entities/forum-boarium]], lying along the triumphal procession route between the Circus Maximus and the Forum. During his 46 BC Gallic triumph, [[entities/julius-caesar]]'s chariot axle broke as he passed through the district, almost throwing him from the vehicle.
