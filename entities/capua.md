# Capua

Capua was the chief city of [[entities/campania]], recaptured by Rome in 210 BC after its revolt during the Second Punic War. The disposition of its spoils of war — submitted to the pontifical college to determine which objects were sacred and which profane — set the precedent the Senate later invoked when handling the disputed Ambracia art taken by [[entities/marcus-fulvius-nobilior]].
