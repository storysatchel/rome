# Haliartus

Haliartus was a city in central Boeotia, besieged twice in 171 BC during the opening year of the Third Macedonian War: first by the legate Publius Lentulus (who was ordered to abandon the siege), then by the praetor Gaius Lucretius, who surrounded it with 10,000 marines and 2,000 allied troops, took the city, and sold its inhabitants into slavery.
