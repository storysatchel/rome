# Quintus Pompeius (cos. 141 BC)

A Roman politician who, when Gaius Laelius planned to run for the consulship, was asked by Scipio Aemilianus whether he himself intended to stand; Pompeius said no and promised to support Laelius. He then ran for the consulship himself and won, breaking his word, which led Scipio to withdraw his friendship from him. Not to be confused with Pompey the Great or with Q. Pompeius Rufus, consul of 88 BC.
