# Sicilia (Roman Province)

Sicily became Rome's first overseas province after the First Punic War (241 BC), administered by an annual praetor or, later, propraetorian governor. Its grain wealth made provincial administration there especially lucrative — and especially prone to the kind of extortion later associated with [[entities/gaius-verres]]'s governorship. Earlier in the province's history it was also the setting of [[entities/cato-the-elder]]'s quaestorship under Scipio Africanus, [[entities/lucius-caecilius-metellus]]'s elephant-filled triumph after defeating Carthaginian forces there, and the legendary Trojan funeral games at [[entities/drepanum]].

## District-Tier Locations

- [[entities/drepanum]] — legendary harbor city, site of the Aeneid's funeral games.
- [[entities/syracuse]] — Greek city used by Scipio Africanus as his staging base for the African invasion.
