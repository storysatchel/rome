# Gaius Cassius Longinus

Co-leader, with Brutus, of the plot that killed Julius Caesar. At the Antium family council of June 44 BC, Cassius arrived mid-discussion and, with a fierce expression, flatly refused the Senate's offer of a grain commission to Sicily, calling it an insult dressed as a favor, and declared he would go to Greece instead.
