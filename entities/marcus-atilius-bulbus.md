# Marcus Atilius Bulbus

M. Atilius Bulbus was an officer serving in Illyria under C. Cosconius between 78 and 76 BC. He incited a mutiny in one of Cosconius's legions for causes the surviving sources do not record. Order was restored and Bulbus was subsequently tried in Rome — one of several cases Cicero cites of officers tried for inciting indiscipline in the army. ^[ambiguous]
