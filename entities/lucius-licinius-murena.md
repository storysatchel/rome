# Lucius Licinius Murena

Elected consul for 62 BC, Murena was prosecuted *de ambitu* (electoral bribery) in November 63 BC by Cato the Younger, who had publicly vowed to prosecute any bribing candidate, together with the defeated candidate Servius Sulpicius Rufus. Cicero defended Murena and won his acquittal despite acknowledging the evidence of bribery, partly by ridiculing Cato's rigid Stoicism in court.
