# Marcus Aemilius Lepidus (Triumvir)

Third and junior member of the Second Triumvirate alongside Mark Antony and Octavian. After contributing forces to the 36 BC campaign against Sextus Pompey in Sicily, Lepidus attempted to claim the island for himself and challenge Octavian's authority, but his troops deserted to Octavian and he was stripped of his triumviral powers, retaining only the ceremonial office of Pontifex Maximus for the rest of his life.
