# Gaius Servilius Glaucia

Praetor in 100 BC and close political ally of the tribune Saturninus. His bid for the consulship of 99 BC was threatened by the strong candidacy of Gaius Memmius; agents of Glaucia and Saturninus murdered Memmius during the election itself. The Senate declared Glaucia a public enemy, and he was dragged from a nearby house and killed by a mob after Marius suppressed the ensuing unrest.
