# Aulus Manlius Vulso

A. Manlius Vulso was consul in 178 BC, assigned Gaul as his province, from which he invaded Histria and was initially defeated. His colleague [[entities/marcus-iunius-brutus-cos177]] had to join him to finish subduing the Histrians. The setback led tribunes [[entities/licinius-nerva-and-papirius-turdus]] to campaign for stripping his prorogued command in 177 BC, a bill ultimately vetoed by a fellow tribune.
