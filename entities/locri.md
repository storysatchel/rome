# Locri

Locri was a Greek-founded port city on the coast of [[entities/bruttium]], seized by [[entities/scipio-africanus]] in 205 BC as a base for his planned African invasion. Scipio's legate [[entities/gaius-pleminius]], left in command there, presided over a garrison regime of such rapacity that his men plundered the city's temple of Proserpina, provoking a mutiny and an embassy of Locrian citizens to the Senate in Rome.
