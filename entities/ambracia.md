# Ambracia

Ambracia, once the seat of King Pyrrhus, was captured by consul [[entities/marcus-fulvius-nobilior]] in 187 BC at the close of the Aetolian War. Fulvius spared the city from sack but carried off hundreds of dedicatory statues from its temples, provoking Ambraciote witnesses to accuse him of sacrilege before the Senate in Rome.
