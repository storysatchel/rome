# The Olive Stump of Faunus

A stripped olive-tree stump on the Laurentine battlefield, sacred to the rustic god Faunus, around which [[entities/aeneas]] chases the fleeing [[entities/turnus]] in [[scenarios/aeneid-aeneas-turnus-final-duel]]. Aeneas's spear sticks fast in its wood, and the delay while he struggles to free it gives [[entities/juturna]] the opening to slip her brother Turnus a fresh sword.
