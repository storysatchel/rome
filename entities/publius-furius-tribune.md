# P. Furius (tr. pl. 100 or 99 BC)

Tribune of the plebs in 100 or 99 BC, prosecuted the following year for his conduct in office. A crowd killed him before his trial before the popular assembly could reach a verdict — one of several violent deaths of tribunes and their allies in the turbulent years following Saturninus's death.
