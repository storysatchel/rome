# Titus Quinctius Flamininus

Consul in 198 BC and censor in 189 BC, celebrated as the general who declared the freedom of the Greeks at the Isthmian Games. When his younger brother Lucius was expelled from the Senate by Cato's censorship, Titus supported him in denying the charge, and later led the senatorial move to annul and re-auction the public contracts Cato and Valerius Flaccus had arranged as censors, and incited a tribune to attempt to fine Cato.
