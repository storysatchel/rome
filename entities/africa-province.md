# Africa (Roman Province)

Rome organized the province of Africa from former Carthaginian territory after the destruction of Carthage in 146 BC (Third Punic War). It bordered, but did not initially include, the independent kingdom of [[entities/numidia]] to its west — the two are frequently conflated in loose usage but remained politically distinct through most of the Republican period. [[entities/lucius-sergius-catilina]] governed it (67–66 BC) and was later tried, collusively, for extorting its communities.
