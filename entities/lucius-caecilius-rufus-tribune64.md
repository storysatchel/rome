# Lucius Caecilius Rufus

L. Caecilius Rufus, tribune of the plebs in 64 BC, proposed a bill soon after taking office to cancel part of the penalty the lex Calpurnia prescribed for ambitus — apparently aimed at easing the consequences for [[entities/publius-cornelius-sulla-consul-designate]] and [[entities/publius-autronius-paetus]].
