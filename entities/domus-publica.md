# Domus Publica (House of the Pontifex Maximus)

The Domus Publica, official residence of the [[entities/julius-caesar|Pontifex Maximus]] near the [[entities/forum-romanum]], hosted the all-female rites of the Bona Dea each December. In December 62 BC [[entities/clodius-pulcher]] disguised himself as a woman to infiltrate the rites here, sparking the sacrilege scandal and trial of 61 BC.
