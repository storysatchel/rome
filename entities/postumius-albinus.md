# Postumius Albinus (murdered former consul)

A former consul serving in some command capacity during the Social War, Postumius Albinus was murdered by his own mutinous troops — nominally under Sulla's ultimate authority — during a prolonged siege in 89 BC. The episode illustrates the breakdown of military discipline and the growing personal loyalty of Roman soldiers to individual commanders like Sulla rather than to the state.
