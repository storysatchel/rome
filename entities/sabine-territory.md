# Sabine Territory

The Sabine country lay in the hills northeast of Rome, home to a people incorporated early into the Roman citizen body by tradition. [[entities/reate]] (modern Rieti), above the Rosea plain, was its principal town referenced in this wiki, its livestock economy dependent on Manius Curius Dentatus's canal draining Lake Velinus.
