# Gaius Lucretius (praetor 171 BC)

C. Lucretius, praetor in 171 BC, was accused by Chalcidian ambassadors of cruelty and robbery against their community. Prosecuted before the people by tribunes [[entities/juventius-thalna-and-aufidius]], he was convicted by all the tribes and fined one million asses.
