# M. Coponius

The testator's nearer blood relative in the *Causa Curiana* (94/93 BC), who sued to claim the estate on the grounds that the will's residuary clause (triggered only if a son was born and died before majority) had never technically been activated, since no son was ever born at all. Represented by Q. Mucius Scaevola; lost to M'. Curius.
