# Venetia

Venetia was the region of northeastern Italy inhabited by the Veneti, containing the towns of [[entities/ateste]] and [[entities/vicetia]], whose 134 BC boundary dispute Rome arbitrated in a pattern similar to the *sententia Minuciorum* between [[entities/genua]] and the Langenses Viturii.
