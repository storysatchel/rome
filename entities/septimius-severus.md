# Septimius Severus

Roman emperor from AD 193 to 211, founder of the Severan dynasty. He emerged victorious from the civil wars following the "Year of the Five Emperors," defeating rival claimant Clodius Albinus at the Battle of Lyon (Lugdunum) in AD 197, after which the city of Lugdunum was penalized for its support of Albinus. He later co-issued, with his son and co-emperor Caracalla, a legal rescript (AD 204) resolving a property dispute involving Jewish merchants.
