# Thermopylae

Thermopylae, the famous narrow pass in central Greece, was the site of consul [[entities/manius-acilius-glabrio]]'s 191 BC victory over King Antiochus III of the Seleucid Empire, with [[entities/cato-the-elder]] serving as a subordinate officer in the campaign. The booty Glabrio took from this victory later became the subject of a tribunician prosecution in Rome.
