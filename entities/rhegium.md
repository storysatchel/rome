# Rhegium

Rhegium was a Greek city at the toe of the Italian peninsula, garrisoned by Rome against Pyrrhus around 280 BC. The garrison, probably composed largely of Campanians under a commander named Decius, mutinied and seized the city for itself. Rome recaptured it and executed the surviving garrison troops by 270 BC, an act that drew a tribune's formal complaint that citizen rights had been violated.
