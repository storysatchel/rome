# Verres's Sicilian Extortion Trial

> [!tldr] A focused scenario for testing provincial accountability through Verres's prosecution in Sicily.

## Scope

Testing provincial accountability through Verres's prosecution in Sicily

## Scenario Evidence

Cicero constructs Verres’ judicial conduct in Sicily as abuse of delegated Roman authority (§§53–60, pp. 23–31) ^f8cb89e7b37bb9d1
The speech contrasts Roman legal ideals with arbitrary provincial coercion and extraction (§§60–68, pp. 31–40) ^f8cb89e7b37bb9d1
Witnesses, documents, and exemplary cases are arranged to make provincial misconduct legible to a Roman audience (§§69–76, pp. 40–49) ^f8cb89e7b37bb9d1
