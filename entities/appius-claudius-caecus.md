# Appius Claudius Caecus

Patrician statesman, censor in 312 BC and consul in 307 and 296 BC, famed for building the Via Appia and the Aqua Appia. His censorship controversially reassigned low-born citizens across the Roman voting tribes, reportedly helping elevate Gnaeus Flavius — son of a freed slave — to the curule aedileship. He opposed granting his rival Quintus Fabius Rullianus a proconsulship in 307 BC, and Rullianus later unwound Claudius's voting-tribe changes during his own censorship of 304 BC.
