# Oropus

Oropus was a town in Boeotia, Greece, home to the temple of Amphiaraus. Sulla had granted the temple's land inviolable, tax-exempt status during his campaigns in Greece. When Roman tax-farmers (publicani) later tried to assess dues on that land, the town successfully petitioned the senate and the consuls of 73 BC for confirmation of its exemption.
