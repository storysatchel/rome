# Publius Rutilius Rufus

A Roman statesman whom Seneca's *On Providence* cites as a case of Fortune "trying" a great man "by exile" — implying an unjust conviction ("those who condemned him will have to defend themselves before all posterity"). Seneca singles out his later refusal of Sulla's invitation to return from exile, preferring to withdraw even further rather than return to Rome under the dictator.
