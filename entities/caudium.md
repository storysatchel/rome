# Caudium

Caudium was a Samnite town in [[entities/samnium]], near the mountain pass known as the Caudine Forks. In 321 BC a Roman consular army was trapped near the town by [[entities/gaius-pontius]]'s forces and forced to surrender, an episode that became a byword for military humiliation.
