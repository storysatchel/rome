# Janiculum

The Janiculum was a hill west of the Tiber in [[entities/rome]], across from the city's traditional seven hills. A ceremonial military flag flew there during meetings of the *comitia centuriata* at the nearby [[entities/campus-martius]] — an archaic reminder of the assembly's origin as an armed muster — and its lowering was a recognized signal halting proceedings. During the 63 BC *perduellio* trial of [[entities/gaius-rabirius]], [[entities/metellus-celer]] pulled the flag down, dissolving the assembly just as it was about to ratify Rabirius's death sentence.
