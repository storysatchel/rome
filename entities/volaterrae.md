# Volaterrae

Volaterrae was an old Etruscan hill town in [[entities/etruria]]. It is attested in this wiki as the home of [[entities/aulus-caecina]], the plaintiff whose armed eviction from a disputed farm near the town produced the civil suit behind Cicero's *Pro Caecina*.
