# C. Sulpicius Galba (sacerdos, d. after 109 BC)

A Roman priest (*sacerdos*, attested by 109 BC), convicted that year by the special *lex Mamilia* commission investigating treasonous collusion with King Jugurtha of Numidia during the early Jugurthine War.
