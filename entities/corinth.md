# Corinth

Corinth was one of the wealthiest and most celebrated cities of the Greek world, part of the Achaean League's territory in [[entities/achaea-greece]]. In 146 BC [[entities/lucius-mummius]] destroyed the city at the conclusion of the Achaean War, razing its buildings, killing much of the male population, and selling the women and children into slavery; its looted art and treasure funded Mummius's [[entities/temple-of-hercules-victor]] at Rome.
