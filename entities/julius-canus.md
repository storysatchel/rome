# Julius Canus

A Roman Stoic described by Seneca as "a man of the first quality," condemned to death by Caligula after "a long altercation" with the emperor. Seneca's *On Tranquillity of Mind* holds him up as a model of Stoic composure for his conduct during the ten days between sentence and execution.
