# Sentinum

Sentinum was a town in [[entities/umbria]], the site of a decisive Roman victory in 295 BC over a combined Samnite-Gallic coalition during the Third Samnite War. The battle is traditionally associated with the ritual self-sacrifice (*devotio*) of the consul [[entities/publius-decius-mus]].
