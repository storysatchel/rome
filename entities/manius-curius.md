# M'. Curius

The named residuary heir in a disputed will, contingent on the testator's son (never actually born) dying before reaching majority. Represented by L. Licinius Crassus in the *Causa Curiana* (94/93 BC), he won the inheritance over the testator's nearer blood relative, M. Coponius, when the court favored the will's evident intent over its literal wording.
