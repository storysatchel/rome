# Floronia (Vestal Virgin, d. 216 BC)

A Vestal Virgin convicted of *incestum* alongside her colleague Opimia shortly after the disaster at Cannae in 216 BC. Rather than face live burial, she took her own life before the sentence could be carried out. Her alleged lover, the pontifical scribe L. Cantilius, was flogged to death in the Comitium by the Pontifex Maximus personally.
