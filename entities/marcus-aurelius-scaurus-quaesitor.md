# M. Aurelius Scaurus (cos. suff. 108 BC)

Suffect consul of 108 BC, one of three *quaesitores* presiding over the special Mamilian Commission's 109 BC series of treason trials against commanders and officials accused of colluding with King Jugurtha. Some ancient sources confuse him with the more famous M. Aemilius Scaurus (cos. 115 BC), a separate individual; modern scholars (per Sumner) argue the Mamilian quaesitor was in fact this Aurelius Scaurus.
