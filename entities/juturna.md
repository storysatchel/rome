# Juturna

Turnus's immortal sister, a water nymph who intervenes on her brother's behalf during the climactic Book XII duel with Aeneas — disguising herself as the charioteer Metiscus and handing Turnus back his sword after he loses his own. Jupiter ultimately orders Juno to withdraw her support, ending the divine interference and sealing Turnus's fate.
