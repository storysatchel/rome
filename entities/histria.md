# Histria

Histria was the peninsula on the northern Adriatic coast (modern Istria), home to the Histri people. In 178 BC the consul [[entities/aulus-manlius-vulso]], assigned [[entities/gaul]] as his province, invaded Histria and suffered an initial defeat before his colleague [[entities/marcus-iunius-brutus-cos177|M. Iunius Brutus]] marched to his aid and the two consular armies broke Histrian resistance. The setback prompted tribunes to seek Manlius's prosecution the following year.
