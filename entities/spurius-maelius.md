# Spurius Maelius

A wealthy plebeian of equestrian status in the mid-5th century BC, Maelius won unbounded popularity by selling grain cheaply and giving much of it away free during a famine. Suspected (on, by Cicero's own account, scant evidence) of using this popularity to make a bid for kingship, he was summoned before the dictator Cincinnatus and, resisting the summons, was killed by Cincinnatus's master of the horse, Gaius Servilius Ahala.
