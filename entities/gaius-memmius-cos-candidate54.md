# Gaius Memmius (consular candidate, 54 BC)

Gaius Memmius was a candidate for the consulship of 53 BC. He and fellow candidate [[entities/gnaeus-domitius-calvinus]] secretly contracted with outgoing consul [[entities/appius-claudius-pulcher]] to produce (if elected) fraudulent augural witnesses attesting to a lex curiata, forfeiting a sum of money if they failed to do so.
