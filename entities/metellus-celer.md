# Quintus Caecilius Metellus Celer

Q. Caecilius Metellus Celer, consul of 60 BC, opposed both the publicans' demands regarding their Asian contracts and the tribune [[entities/tribune-flavius-60bc]]'s agrarian bill for [[entities/pompey]]'s veterans. Flavius responded by threatening to block Metellus Celer from his province and briefly imprisoning him, but the consul's resistance ultimately defeated the bill. Three years earlier, in 63 BC, he had pulled down the ceremonial military flag flown over the Janiculum hill during the assembly convened to ratify Gaius Rabirius's death sentence for *perduellio* — a traditional signal whose removal automatically dissolved the assembly and halted the proceedings before Rabirius could be formally condemned.
