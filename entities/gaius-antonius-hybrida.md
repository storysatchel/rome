# Gaius Antonius Hybrida

Uncle of Mark Antony and later consul of 63 BC (Cicero's colleague), Antonius Hybrida was prosecuted *de repetundis* by the young Julius Caesar in 76 BC, like Cn. Dolabella shortly before him, becoming one of Caesar's early oratorical targets. He escaped conviction at that time, though he was later convicted and exiled for misconduct during his Macedonian proconsulship.
