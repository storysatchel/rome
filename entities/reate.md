# Reate

Reate (modern Rieti) was a Sabine town in central Italy whose nearby Rosea plain, reclaimed from swampland by [[entities/manius-curius-dentatus]]'s canal draining Lake Velinus, supported an important livestock economy. Reate's dependence on the canal placed it in a long-running dispute with downstream Interamna, ultimately argued before a Roman senatorial commission with [[entities/cicero]] as Reate's advocate.
