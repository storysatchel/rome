# Volcens

Leader of a 300-strong Rutulian cavalry patrol returning from Latinus's city who spots Euryalus's moonlit helmet in the woods and surrounds the two Trojan raiders. When he cannot find the hidden Nisus, who has been killing his men with javelins from cover, Volcens kills the captured Euryalus outright — and is immediately cut down by the vengeful Nisus.
