# Amatius ("the False Marius")

An ambitious oculist (eye-doctor) who, in 44 BC, falsely claimed to be a grandson of Gaius Marius. The claim met with a warm popular reception among the urban plebs and in various Italian towns, trading on enduring nostalgia for Marius. After Caesar's assassination, Amatius attempted to dedicate an altar on the spot in the Forum where Caesar's body had been cremated, exploiting the same wave of popular grief that fueled anti-conspirator riots.^[inferred: the source does not detail his ultimate fate.]
