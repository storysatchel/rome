# Gaius Flavius Fimbria

C. Flavius Fimbria served as quaestor to the consul L. Valerius Flaccus during the campaign against Mithridates. After Flaccus dismissed him over accusations of criminality and undermining authority, Fimbria incited the army to mutiny; Flaccus was killed and Fimbria took command of the legions himself.
