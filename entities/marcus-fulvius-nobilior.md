# Marcus Fulvius Nobilior

Roman consul who completed the Aetolian War in 187 BC by capturing Ambracia, once the seat of King Pyrrhus. He spared the city from sacking but carried off large quantities of dedicatory sculptures and paintings, displaying 785 bronze and 230 marble statues in his triumph. Political rivals (*inimici*) sought to block his triumph by prosecuting him on testimony from summoned Ambraciote witnesses, who accused him of sacrilegiously stripping every temple in the city. A man of notable religious learning (he wrote a treatise on the Roman religious calendar), Fulvius ultimately had the Senate refer the disputed artworks to the pontifical college to sort sacred from profane items, as had been done with the spoils of Capua in 210 BC.
