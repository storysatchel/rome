# Campania

Campania was the fertile coastal region south of Latium, home to Capua, [[entities/neapolis-naples]], [[entities/liternum]], [[entities/pompeii]], and numerous other Italian towns. Its proximity to Rome and its own substantial population made it both a recruiting ground (furnishing units like the Legio Campana) and, at various points, a theater of political and military crisis in its own right.

## District-Tier Locations

- [[entities/liternum]] — coastal town, site of Scipio Africanus's self-imposed exile.
- [[entities/pompeii]] — town besieged during the Social War, site of the murder of Postumius Albinus.
- [[entities/capua]] — Campania's chief city, whose 210 BC art-spoils precedent was later applied to Fulvius Nobilior's Ambracia plunder.
