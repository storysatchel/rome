# Aemilia (Vestal Virgin, condemned 114 BC)

A Vestal Virgin accused by an informing slave of incestum in December 114 BC and condemned in the pontifical college's initial trial — the only one of three accused Vestals to be convicted the first time around. Reportedly the lover of T. Betucius Barrus, whose slave brought the accusation.
