# Campus Sceleratus

The Campus Sceleratus, literally the "field of wickedness," lay beside the [[entities/colline-gate]] in [[entities/rome]]. It was the designated underground execution site for [[entities/vestal-virgins]] convicted of *incestum* (unchastity): the condemned priestess was lowered alive into a small underground chamber there and the chamber sealed over. [[entities/minucia-vestal-337-bc]] was buried alive there in 337 BC, and [[entities/opimia-vestal-216-bc]] in 216 BC.
