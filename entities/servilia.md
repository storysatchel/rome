# Servilia

Half-sister of Cato the Younger and mother of Marcus Brutus, Servilia was a politically formidable Roman matron with a long-rumored intimate relationship with Julius Caesar. In June 44 BC, after Caesar's assassination, she hosted and effectively chaired a family council (*consilium*) at her villa in Antium — attended by her daughter Junia Tertia, her niece Porcia, Cicero, Favonius, Brutus, and Cassius — to decide whether the conspirators should leave Italy. She promised to have the Senate's grain commission offer rescinded to spare Cassius the insult of a diminished post.
