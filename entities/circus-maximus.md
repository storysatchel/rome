# Circus Maximus

The Circus Maximus was Rome's principal venue for chariot racing and large-scale public games (*ludi*), laid out in the long valley between the [[entities/palatine-hill]] and [[entities/aventine-hill]]. It is tracked here as the location-of-record for athletic-domain scenarios involving public games and races at Rome itself (as distinct from athletic contests set outside the city, such as the funeral games narrated in the *Aeneid*).
