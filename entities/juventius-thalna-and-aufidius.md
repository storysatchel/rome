# M'. Juventius Thalna and Cn. Aufidius

M'. Juventius Thalna and Cn. Aufidius, tribunes of the plebs, prosecuted [[entities/gaius-lucretius-praetor171]] before the comitia tributa on charges of theft and wrongful injury against the Chalcidians, securing his conviction and a fine of one million asses.
