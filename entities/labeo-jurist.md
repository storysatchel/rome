# Labeo (Jurist, Nola–Naples Arbitrator)

A Roman jurist, recorded in Cicero's *De Officiis* and Valerius Maximus, asked to judge a land dispute between the cities of Nola and Naples. Rather than awarding the land to either disputant, Labeo ruled that it belonged to the Roman state, converting it into *ager publicus*.
