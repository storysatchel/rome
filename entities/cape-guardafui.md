# Cape Guardafui

Cape Guardafui is a promontory on the Horn of Africa, near where [[entities/eudoxus-of-cyzicus]] landed and made contact with local peoples after misjudging the monsoon winds on his return voyage from [[entities/india]].^[inferred] The encounter advanced Greek geographic knowledge of the East African coastline.
