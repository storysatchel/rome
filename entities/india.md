# India (Ancient)

India was the destination of [[entities/eudoxus-of-cyzicus]]'s two pioneering sea voyages from the [[entities/red-sea]] around 120 BC, guided by a rescued Tamil-speaking shipwreck survivor who knew the monsoon wind patterns. The spice cargo Eudoxus brought back from India was confiscated both times by [[entities/ptolemy-viii]]'s customs officials in [[entities/alexandria]].
