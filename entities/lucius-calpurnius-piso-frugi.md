# Lucius Calpurnius Piso Frugi

Consul in 133 BC and an early Roman annalistic historian, Piso earned the cognomen "Frugi" ("the Honest/Thrifty One") for his reputation of integrity. In 123/122 BC he became a vocal opponent of Gaius Gracchus's grain law (*lex frumentaria*), publicly needling Gracchus by showing up to collect the subsidized grain despite his wealth and his opposition to the law.
