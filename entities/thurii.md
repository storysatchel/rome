Thurii was a town in southern Italy near [[entities/bruttium]], still unsettled from its occupation by Spartacus's slave army in 72 BC when the bordering landowners [[entities/marcus-tullius-plaintiff]] and [[entities/publius-fabius-litigant]] clashed violently over their adjoining properties in 71 BC.
