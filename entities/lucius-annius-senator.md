# L. Annius (senator, expelled 307 BCE)

L. Annius was a Roman senator struck from the Senate rolls by the censors of 307 BCE (447 AUC) for divorcing his wife *nullo amicorum consilio adhibito* — without first convening the customary council of friends — an improper procedure for a man of his rank even though divorce itself was lawful.
