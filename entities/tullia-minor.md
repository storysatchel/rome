# Tullia Minor

Daughter of the Roman king Servius Tullius, in the legendary regal-period tradition transmitted by Livy and Ovid. Tullia incited her husband Lucius Tarquinius to seize the throne by having her father murdered, and then drove her carpentum over his corpse in the street afterward named the Vicus Sceleratus in memory of the act.
