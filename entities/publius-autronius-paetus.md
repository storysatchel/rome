# P. Autronius Paetus (cos. des. 65 BC)

Elected consul for 65 BC, but convicted of electoral bribery (*ambitus*) under the *lex Calpurnia* before taking office, prosecuted by his defeated rival L. Aurelius Cotta, who assumed the consulship in his place. Ancient sources connect his resulting bitterness to subsequent conspiratorial violence associated with Catiline.
