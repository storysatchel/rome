# Euryalus

Described by Virgil as "exceptional for beauty and bloom of youth," Euryalus is Nisus's beloved companion. He wins the Book V foot race after Nisus trips a rival for him, and dies in Book IX, captured and killed by Volcens's cavalry patrol during their night raid, his death provoking Nisus's own suicidal final charge.
