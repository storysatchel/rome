# Statius Albius Oppianicus

An equestrian defendant convicted in the 74 BC *iudicium Iunianum* of repeated poisoning attempts, principally against his stepson A. Cluentius Habitus, whose mother Oppianicus had married. The jury's bribery-tainted narrow verdict against him became one of the Late Republic's most notorious legal scandals, chiefly known through Cicero's later *Pro Cluentio*.
