# Rosea Plain

The Rosea plain lay below [[entities/lake-velinus]] near [[entities/reate]], periodically flooded until [[entities/manius-curius-dentatus]]'s canal drained the lake and reclaimed the valley as pastureland underpinning Reate's livestock economy. Cicero, pleading Reate's cause, called it "their Tempe" in his letter describing the [[scenarios/reate-interamna-velinus-lake-dispute|Reate–Interamna dispute]].
