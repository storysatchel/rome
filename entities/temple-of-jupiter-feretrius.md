# Temple of Jupiter Feretrius

The Temple of Jupiter Feretrius stood on the [[entities/capitoline-hill]] and was the traditional repository of the *spolia opima*, the armor stripped from an enemy commander killed in single combat by a Roman supreme commander. [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus]] dedicated the armor of [[entities/viridomarus]] there in 222 BC, only the third such dedication in Roman history.
