# Titus Labienus

Tribune of the plebs in 63 BC and nephew of the tribune Saturninus, killed in 100 BC. Labienus prosecuted the elderly senator Gaius Rabirius under the revived archaic charge of *perduellio* for Saturninus's death, theatrically displaying Saturninus's funeral imago (ancestor mask) during the trial to provoke the crowd. He later served as one of Julius Caesar's chief lieutenants in Gaul before defecting to Pompey in the civil war.
