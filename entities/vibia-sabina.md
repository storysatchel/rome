# Vibia Sabina

Vibia Sabina was the wife of the emperor [[entities/hadrian|Hadrian]] and a great-niece of Trajan. Her place in the imperial family linked Hadrian to Trajan's household before his accession. As *Augusta*, she accompanied the emperor on journeys and appeared in the visual culture of his reign.

## Family and marriage

Sabina was the daughter of Salonia Matidia and Lucius Vibius Sabinus. Her grandmother Marciana was Trajan's sister. In AD 100 she married Hadrian; the marriage brought together the Ulpian and Aelian families at a time when Trajan had no children.[^family] A later ancient account credits Trajan's wife [[Plotina]] with promoting the marriage, while reporting that Trajan himself was less enthusiastic. That account's portrayal of influence at court should not be treated as a direct record of private negotiations.[^marriage]

Hadrian became emperor in 117. Sabina and Hadrian had no children. Surviving hostile stories about their relationship and her alleged feelings cannot establish her private motives with certainty.[^relationship]

## Augusta and the imperial journey

Sabina received the honorific title *Augusta* and travelled with Hadrian.[^augusta] In Egypt in 130, the imperial couple's arrival at Alexandria was commemorated on a coin showing Isis and Serapis greeting them. Other Alexandrian coins of 130–131 represented Sabina in association with Isis, Athena, and Eusebia. These images show how her public position could be expressed through local religious and civic imagery; they do not by themselves reveal her personal religious beliefs.[^coinage]

At [[entities/thebes-egypt|Thebes]], an inscription commemorated Sabina hearing the [[entities/colossus-of-memnon|Colossus of Memnon]] twice during the first hour after sunrise. Casson reads verses by a poet in the imperial entourage as suggesting that the statue did not sound for Hadrian on an occasion when he visited; that is an interpretation, not proof of the emperor's reaction.[^memnon] Sabina's friend [[entities/julia-balbilla|Julia Balbilla]] had joined Hadrian's eastward journey from Athens in 129.[^balbilla] The Memnon episode is explored in [[scenarios/hadrian-silent-memnon]].

## Death and remembrance

Sabina died before Hadrian, late in his reign, and was deified by the senate. Ancient rumours about her death and the state of the marriage are not secure evidence for their causes.[^death] Her deification was distinct from the divine associations used in imagery during her lifetime.[^deification]
