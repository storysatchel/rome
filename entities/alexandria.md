# Alexandria

Alexandria was the Ptolemaic royal capital and chief port of [[entities/egypt]]. A shipwrecked Indian sailor was brought to the Ptolemaic court there around 120 BC, leading [[entities/eudoxus-of-cyzicus]] to launch pioneering voyages to [[entities/india]] — voyages whose returning cargo was twice confiscated by [[entities/ptolemy-viii]]'s customs officials there. Centuries later it was also the departure point for [[entities/synesius-of-cyrene]]'s harrowing AD 404 coastal voyage toward [[entities/cyrene]].
