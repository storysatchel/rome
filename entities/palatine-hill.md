# Palatine Hill

The Palatine was [[entities/rome|Rome]]'s most prestigious residential hill, traditionally identified as the site of Rome's original founding and, by the late Republic, home to the town houses of many of the city's most senior senatorial families. Its valley toward the [[entities/circus-maximus|Circus Maximus]] separated it from the Aventine. Individual named houses on the Palatine are tracked as building-tier entities as specific scenarios require them.
