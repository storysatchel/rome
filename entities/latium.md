# Latium

Latium was the region of west-central Italy inhabited by the Latin peoples, of whom the Romans were originally one community among many. It contained Rome itself along with the other old Latin League towns — [[entities/tusculum]], [[entities/aricia]], [[entities/ardea]], [[entities/bovillae]], and others — bound to Rome and each other by shared language, cult, and (before Rome's conquest of the League) a loose federal structure. Most of this wiki's Rome-adjacent scenarios and entities nest under Latium. In Virgil's *Aeneid*, Latium is also the legendary setting of King Latinus's kingdom of [[entities/laurentum]] and the war between the Trojans and Rutulians fought on its ground.
