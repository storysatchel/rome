# M. Aemilius Scaurus (cos. 115 BC)

Consul in 115 BC, censor in 109 BC, and for decades the Senate's *princeps senatus* (first-ranked senator) — one of the dominant figures of Late Republican politics. He was tried for extortion repeatedly across his career (cases spanning the 110s to 90s BC), most memorably in a mutual, back-to-back prosecution feud with his rival Q. Servilius Caepio in 92/91 BC, in which each tried to have the other tried first.
