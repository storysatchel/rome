# Viridomarus

Viridomarus (Viridomar) was king of the Insubrian Gauls, defeated and killed in single combat by the Roman consul M. Claudius Marcellus at the battle of Clastidium in 222 BC. His death and the stripping of his arms furnished Marcellus with only the third *spolia opima* in Roman tradition, after Romulus and Cornelius Cossus.
