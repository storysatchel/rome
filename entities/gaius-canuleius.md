# C. Canuleius

A tribune of the plebs, probably in 99 BC, possibly a co-prosecutor (or a separate, second prosecutor) of P. Furius in the case that ended with Furius's lynching before judgment. Ancient sources disagree on whether Canuleius is a distinct historical figure from Appian's confused reference to "Appuleius," or a genuine separate prosecutor.
