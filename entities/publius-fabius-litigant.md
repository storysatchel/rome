# P. Fabius (litigant, 71 BC)

Landowner near Thurii accused by his neighbor M. Tullius of sending armed men to attack his property and slaves in 71 BC, shortly after the region's occupation by Spartacus's forces had ended. Defended by L. Quinctius against Tullius's suit (argued by Cicero) for violent property damage.
