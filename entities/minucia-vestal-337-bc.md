# Minucia (Vestal Virgin, d. 337 BC)

A Vestal from a prominent plebeian family, first placed under informal restriction by the pontifices for allegedly dressing and behaving too elegantly, then formally accused of incestum on a slave's testimony. She was convicted and buried alive at the Campus Sceleratus in 337 BC — the same year as Rome's first plebeian praetorship.
