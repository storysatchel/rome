# A. Cluentius Habitus

An equestrian whose stepfather, Statius Albius Oppianicus, repeatedly attempted to poison him; Cluentius brought the 74 BC *iudicium Iunianum* prosecution that convicted Oppianicus. Years later, Cluentius himself faced a belated accusation that he had bribed the original jury to secure that conviction — a charge Cicero's *Pro Cluentio* (66 BC) successfully rebutted.
