# A. Caecina (of Volaterrae)

An *eques* from Volaterrae in Etruria, forcibly prevented by an armed gang from taking possession of disputed land, allegedly organized by Sex. Aebutius. He sued under the interdict for violent dispossession, with Cicero as his advocate in the surviving *Pro Caecina* — one of Cicero's most technically detailed civil-law speeches.
