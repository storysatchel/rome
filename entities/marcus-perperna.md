# Marcus Perperna

A Roman officer serving under the rebel general Quintus Sertorius in Hispania, Perperna organized the conspiracy that murdered Sertorius in 72 BC, apparently out of jealousy and ambition to take command himself. He was defeated and executed by Pompey shortly afterward.
