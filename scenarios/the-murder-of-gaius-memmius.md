# The Murder of Gaius Memmius During the Consular Election of 99 BC

In 99 BC the tribune Lucius Appuleius Saturninus, already elected to an unprecedented third tribunate, backed his ally Gaius Servilius Glaucia's bid for the consulship. One consular seat was filled without difficulty, but a second candidate, Gaius Memmius, had a strong chance of winning the other seat — which would have shut Glaucia out entirely.

## Parties & Stakes

- **Gaius Memmius** ([[entities/gaius-memmius-99bc]]) — goal: win the second consular seat for 99 BC; resources: evidently strong popular support, enough to threaten Glaucia's candidacy.
- **Gaius Servilius Glaucia** ([[entities/gaius-servilius-glaucia]]) — goal: secure the consulship by eliminating his chief rival; resources: praetorian rank, hired agents, alliance with Saturninus.
- **Lucius Appuleius Saturninus** ([[entities/lucius-appuleius-saturninus]]) — goal: protect his ally Glaucia's candidacy and consolidate their joint political faction; resources: tribunician office, hired agents, Marius's veterans massed nearby (from an earlier, separate confrontation).
- **Gaius Marius** ([[entities/marius]]) — role: former ally of Saturninus and Glaucia, compelled by the Senate to suppress them after the murder.

## What Happened

With the election for the second consular seat actually underway, hired agents of Saturninus and Glaucia beat Gaius Memmius to death in order to remove him as a rival candidate. ^[extracted] The Senate responded the next day by declaring both Saturninus and Glaucia public enemies and calling on Marius — their former political ally — to defend the republic against them. ^[extracted] After a pitched battle in the [[entities/forum-romanum|Forum]] dated to 10 December 100 BC, Marius captured Saturninus and his followers on the [[entities/capitoline-hill|Capitol]] and, despite assurances of safety, Saturninus was stoned to death in the [[entities/curia-hostilia|Curia Hostilia]]; Glaucia was dragged from a nearby house and also killed. This dating conflicts with the placement of the election account in 99 BC above. ^[extracted]

## Outcome

Memmius's murder directly destroyed Saturninus and Glaucia's political faction: both men were killed within days by a mob despite official guarantees of safety, and Marius, disgruntled at having to turn on his former allies, left [[entities/rome|Rome]] for the East in a kind of retirement. The affair had a long legal postscript decades later in the trial of Gaius Rabirius (see [[scenarios/the-trial-of-gaius-rabirius-for-perduellio]]).

## Open Questions

- The sequence remains chronologically unresolved here: a battle dated to December 100 BC follows an election account placed in 99 BC. ^[ambiguous]
- The identities and fates of the hired agents who actually killed Memmius are not recorded.
