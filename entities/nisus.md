# Nisus

Son of Hyrtacus, a Trojan warrior whose devoted love for the younger Euryalus drives both his foot-race foul (tripping a rival to secure Euryalus's win) and his death in Book IX, where he dies avenging his companion after a night raid on the sleeping Rutulian camp goes wrong.
