# Aricia

A Latin city near Rome. In 446 BC, Aricia and neighboring Ardea were unable to resolve a dispute over possession of a tract of land and jointly asked Rome to arbitrate. When Rome could not reach a decision, it declared the disputed land its own public property (*ager publicus*), to the detriment of both petitioning cities.
