# Manius Acilius Glabrio

Consul in 191 BC, Glabrio commanded Roman forces to victory over Antiochus III at Thermopylae. In early 189 BC he was accused by two tribunes of giving an inadequate account of the booty from his campaign; Cato, his former subordinate in that campaign, appeared as a prominent prosecution witness. Glabrio was also one of Cato's rivals in the 189 BC censorial election.
