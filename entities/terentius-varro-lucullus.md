# Marcus Terentius Varro Lucullus

M. Terentius Varro Lucullus was consul in 73 BC, serving alongside C. Cassius Longinus. He is distinct from his more famous adoptive brother [[entities/lucius-licinius-lucullus]]. As consul, he co-signed a letter to the Boeotian town of Oropus confirming a senatorial decree protecting the town's sacred, tax-exempt land from assessment by Roman tax-farmers.
