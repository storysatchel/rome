# Servius Tullius

The sixth legendary king of Rome. According to Roman tradition, he was overthrown and murdered by his son-in-law Lucius Tarquinius (Tarquinius Superbus), at the instigation of his own daughter Tullia, who afterward drove her carriage over his corpse.
