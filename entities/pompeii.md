# Pompeii (Ancient City)

Pompeii was a town in [[entities/campania]] besieged by Roman forces under [[entities/sulla]] during the Social War. During the 89 BC siege, mutinous troops nominally under Sulla's command murdered the former consul [[entities/postumius-albinus]]; Sulla deliberately left the killers unpunished.
