# Quintus Sertorius

A Roman general aligned with the Marian faction in the civil wars of the 80s BC, Sertorius established a long-lived independent power base in Hispania after Sulla's victory in Rome, rallying Roman exiles and Iberian allies and resisting several Roman commanders, including Pompey, for nearly a decade until his assassination in 72 BC.
