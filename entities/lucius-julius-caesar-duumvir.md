# Lucius Julius Caesar (duumvir, 63 BC)

Co-appointed with Julius Caesar as one of the two *duumviri* (special commissioners) to try Gaius Rabirius under the revived archaic charge of *perduellio* in 63 BC — a procedure the source describes as "a particularly ancient" one by then rarely used.
