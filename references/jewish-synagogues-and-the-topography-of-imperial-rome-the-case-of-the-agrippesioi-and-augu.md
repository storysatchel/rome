- The Agrippesioi and Augustesioi are known mainly through inscriptions from Rome’s Monteverde Catacombs.   - Conventional interpretation names them directly for Marcus Agrippa and Augustus and assumes imperial founding or patronage.   - Chronology, epigraphic forms, and catacomb evidence challenge the conventional first-century dating and direct-naming assumption.   - Linguistic comparanda and association naming patterns are needed to interpret synagogue identities.   - Topographical analysis locates candidate synagogue communities in Rome while retaining uncertainty about precise sites. ^[ambiguous]
