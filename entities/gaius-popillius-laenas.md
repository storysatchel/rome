# Gaius Popillius Laenas

A Roman senator sent alone, without an army, to intercept King Antiochus IV Epiphanes of the Seleucid Empire as his forces marched to attack Ptolemaic Egypt in 168 BC. Armed only with the authority of the Senate, Popillius delivered an ultimatum demanding Antiochus withdraw immediately — and Antiochus, intimidated by Rome's power, complied.
