# Gaius Memmius (consular candidate, d. 99 BC)

A candidate for the consulship of 99 BC with a good chance of being elected, which would have eliminated Gaius Servilius Glaucia's chances at the second consular seat. Hired agents of Glaucia and the tribune Saturninus beat Memmius to death while the election was actually taking place, triggering the Senate's declaration of Saturninus and Glaucia as public enemies.
