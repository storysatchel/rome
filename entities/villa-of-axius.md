# Villa of Axius

The villa of Axius, near [[entities/reate]], hosted [[entities/cicero]] and [[entities/appius-claudius-pulcher]] on their way to the senatorial hearing over Lake Velinus. Varro's *De Re Rustica* III depicts Appius Claudius joking with fellow aviary-enthusiasts there about senators' bird-themed cognomina, in an aside to the [[scenarios/reate-interamna-velinus-lake-dispute|Reate–Interamna dispute]].
