# Publius Cornelius Sulla (consul-designate 65 BC)

P. Cornelius Sulla, a relative of the dictator [[entities/sulla]], won the consular election for 65 BC together with [[entities/publius-autronius-paetus]]. Both were convicted of electoral bribery (*ambitus*) under the lex Calpurnia, losing the consulship and their senate seats.
