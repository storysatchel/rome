# Cephallenia

Cephallenia was an island in the Ionian Sea that served as the base for the Roman fleet commanded by the praetor [[entities/gaius-lucretius-praetor171]] at the outset of the Third Macedonian War in 171 BC, before his operations against [[entities/haliartus]] and other Boeotian cities.
