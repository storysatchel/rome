# Lake Velinus

Lake Velinus sat above [[entities/reate]] in Sabine territory; its limestone-rich waters periodically flooded the fertile [[entities/rosea-plain|Rosea plain]] below. In 272 BCE [[entities/manius-curius-dentatus]] cut an artificial channel draining the lake into the [[entities/river-nar|river Nar]], creating the falls now called the Cascate delle Marmore and setting off the two-century [[scenarios/reate-interamna-velinus-lake-dispute|Reate–Interamna dispute]].
