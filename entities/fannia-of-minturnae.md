# Fannia (of Minturnae)

A divorced woman from the Latin town of Minturnae who sued her ex-husband, C. Titinius, for the return of her dowry around 100 BC after he invoked a provision allowing a husband to retain part of a dowry if his wife's misconduct caused the divorce. The future six-time consul [[entities/marius]], serving as the case's juror, awarded her the dowry in full save for a single symbolic sesterce.
