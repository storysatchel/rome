# Spurius Postumius (Caudine Forks consul)

One of the two Roman consuls commanding the army trapped by the Samnites at the Caudine Forks in 321 BC; negotiated the capitulation and the army's humiliating passage under the yoke, and was later reportedly offered back to the Samnites by the Senate when it repudiated the peace terms he had agreed to.
