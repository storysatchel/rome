# Sextus Roscius of Ameria

A landowner from the town of Ameria, Sextus Roscius was falsely accused of murdering his own father so that his property could be seized and sold off cheaply during Sulla's proscriptions. A young Marcus Tullius Cicero took on his defense in 80 BC, his first major public oration, and won an acquittal.
