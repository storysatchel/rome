# Lucius Calpurnius Piso Caesoninus

Consul in 58 BC (the year of Cicero's exile) and father of Caesar's wife Calpurnia, Piso Caesoninus governed Macedonia and was later recalled at Cicero's instigation. Cicero's savage *In Pisonem* invective of 55 BC prompted a written reply from Piso circulated as a pamphlet in 54 BC, continuing a multi-year public feud. He went on to become censor in 50 BC. Not to be confused with L. Calpurnius Piso Frugi (cos. 133 BC).
