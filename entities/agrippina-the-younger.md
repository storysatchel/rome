# Agrippina the Younger

Daughter of Germanicus and Agrippina the Elder, wife of the emperor Claudius and mother of the future emperor Nero. Tacitus records that she was granted the unprecedented honor of riding a carpentum (a ceremonial carriage normally reserved for priests) up to the Capitol, which he presents as evidence of her excessive personal power within Claudius's household.
