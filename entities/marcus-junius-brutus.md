# Marcus Junius Brutus

Brutus wrote a treatise *On Virtue*, now lost, describing his visit to the exiled Marcellus at Mytilene. In the account preserved by Seneca, Brutus felt that he himself was departing into exile by leaving his admired friend behind. After participating in Caesar's assassination, Brutus attended a tense family council at his mother Servilia's villa at Antium, alongside [[entities/gaius-cassius-longinus|Cassius]] and Cicero, to debate the conspirators' political strategy. These episodes show his philosophical friendships and the family setting of his political deliberations.
