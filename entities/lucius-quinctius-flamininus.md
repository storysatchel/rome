# Lucius Quinctius Flamininus

Consul in 192 BC and younger brother of Titus Quinctius Flamininus, the "liberator of Greece." While commanding in Cisalpine Gaul, he allegedly executed a condemned criminal (or, per Livy's version, a Boian chieftain who had come seeking Roman protection) merely to gratify a banquet companion, probably a boy named Philippus. Cato, as censor in 184 BC, expelled him from the Senate over the affair.
