# Chalcis

Chalcis was the principal city of [[entities/euboea]]. In 171 BC its ambassadors brought a complaint to the Roman senate accusing the praetor [[entities/gaius-lucretius-praetor171]] of cruelty and robbery against their community, likely connected to his naval command in the opening phase of the Third Macedonian War. Tribunes subsequently prosecuted Lucretius before the people on the Chalcidians' behalf. Chalcis also appears as the destination of a detachment Lucretius sent under his brother Marcus during the same campaign's siege of [[entities/haliartus]].
