# The Publicii (Plebeian Aediles, 241 BC)

Plebeian aediles of 241 BC, mentioned by Ovid, who brought the first prosecution in a long while against graziers who had been illicitly running their herds on public pastures (*ager publicus*) beyond legal limits. Before their intervention, Ovid says, "the masses lacked champions to protect public land."
