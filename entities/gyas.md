# Gyas

Trojan captain of the Chimaera, the largest ship in the Book V funeral-games boat race. Leading at the turning rock, he flies into a rage at his cautious helmsman Menoetes's wide course and throws him overboard, taking the tiller himself — a decision that costs him the race.
