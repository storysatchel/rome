# Synesius of Cyrene

Late antique Greek philosopher from Cyrene (later bishop of Ptolemais), active around AD 400. He wrote a vivid, entertaining letter to his brother describing a harrowing AD 404 sea voyage from Alexandria toward Cyrene aboard a poorly equipped ship run by a debt-ridden captain, Amarantus.
