# G. Iunius (aed. 75 BC)

Curule aedile in 75 BC, who as *iudex quaestionis* presided over the 74 BC trial of Statius Albius Oppianicus — known to history as the *iudicium Iunianum* after him. He was later convicted by the popular assembly for failing to properly swear in or seat a juror during that very trial, part of the broader fallout from the case's bribery scandal.
