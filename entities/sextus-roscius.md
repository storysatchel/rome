# Sextus Roscius of Ameria

A landowner from Ameria, Sextus Roscius was accused of murdering his father and defended by the young Marcus Tullius Cicero in 80 BC. Cicero portrayed the charge as a scheme to seize Roscius's property and sell it cheaply during Sulla's proscriptions. Roscius was acquitted; the alleged conspiracy should be distinguished from the outcome of the trial. The defense was Cicero's first major public oration.
