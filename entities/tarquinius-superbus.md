# Tarquinius Superbus

The seventh and last legendary king of Rome. Incited by his wife Tullia, he seized the throne by having his father-in-law King Servius Tullius murdered. His subsequent tyrannical rule, and the rape of Lucretia by his son Sextus, precipitated the overthrow of the Roman monarchy and the founding of the Republic (traditionally dated to 510/509 BC).
