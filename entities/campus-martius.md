# Campus Martius

The Campus Martius ("Field of Mars") was the open plain outside Rome's sacred boundary (*pomerium*) where the *comitia centuriata* — the assembly organized by military-style "centuries" and used to elect consuls and praetors and declare war — traditionally met, since a body with military characteristics could not, by custom, meet within the city proper. It also served as a mustering ground for armies and the staging point for triumphal processions before they entered the city.
