# Asia (Roman Province)

The province of Asia, in western Anatolia (modern Turkey), came to Rome via the testamentary bequest of King Attalus III of Pergamon in 133 BC. One of the Republic's wealthiest provinces, it later became the staging ground for the Mithridatic Wars and the base of operations for commanders such as [[entities/lucius-licinius-lucullus]]. [[entities/publius-rutilius-rufus]]'s provincial administration curbing corrupt tax-farmers there is traditionally linked to his later conviction and exile, though Seneca's own account does not name the province directly. ^[inferred] Within the province, [[entities/mytilene]] on Lesbos was the site of [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus-cos-51-bce]]'s voluntary civil-war exile.
