# Q. Hortensius Hortalus (cos. 69 BC)

Consul in 69 BC and, before Cicero's rise, Rome's most celebrated courtroom orator, appearing as advocate in dozens of Late Republican trials. He co-defended Cn. Cornelius Dolabella against the young Julius Caesar's 77 BC extortion prosecution, alongside C. Aurelius Cotta.
