# Lucius Mummius

Roman consul and general who commanded the final campaign of the Achaean War, destroying the city of Corinth in 146 BC, slaughtering much of its male population, selling its women and children into slavery, and seizing enormous quantities of plunder — some of which he used to fund a temple to Hercules at Rome.
