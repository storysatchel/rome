# Capitoline Hill

The Capitoline, smallest of Rome's traditional hills, housed the [[entities/temple-of-jupiter-feretrius]] and the Temple of Jupiter Optimus Maximus — the Republic's chief state cult site and the terminal destination of a triumphing general's procession. Its citadel-like defensibility also made it a point of last refuge in political crises, including [[entities/lucius-appuleius-saturninus]]'s final stand before Marius besieged him there in 100 BC.
