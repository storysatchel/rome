# Lucius Scribonius Libo

A Roman senator who was denounced to the censors by Helvius Mancia and subsequently put on trial in 55 BC. Pompey appeared on Libo's behalf and attempted to discredit Mancia, which backfired into a public attack on Pompey's own record.
