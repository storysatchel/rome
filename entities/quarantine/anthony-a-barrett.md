# Anthony A. Barrett

Anthony A. Barrett is the author of *Livia: First Lady of Imperial Rome* (Yale University Press, 2002). His study reconstructs the public standing, dynastic role, and historical memory of Livia Drusilla, consort of Augustus and mother of Tiberius.

## Livia and the Early Principate

The historical Livia was more complex than the hostile caricatures preserved in Tacitus and later imperial historiography. Her career spanned the establishment and consolidation of the Principate: she maintained extensive patronage networks, intervened in legal and civic disputes, and provided dynastic continuity across the transition from Augustus to Tiberius.

Her position was politically influential while remaining constitutionally undefined. While Augustus elevated her public profile through religious restorations, public statues, and sacrosanctity, Tiberius actively resisted formal institutionalization of her authority. Pervasive allegations in ancient sources of poisoning rivals to advance Tiberius remain historically unsubstantiated, reflecting standard rhetorical tropes regarding female intrigue in autocracies.
