# Publius Rutilius Rufus

Rutilius Rufus was a Roman statesman who was convicted and exiled, then later refused Sulla's invitation to return to Rome, preferring to withdraw even further rather than live under the dictator. The conviction's reputation as unjust made his exile an example of principled endurance. Seneca casts Fortune as "trying" him "by exile" and declares that "those who condemned him will have to defend themselves before all posterity."
