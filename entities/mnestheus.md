# Mnestheus

Trojan captain of the Seabeast in the Book V ship race, whom Virgil identifies as the legendary progenitor of the Roman clan Memmius. He finishes second in the boat race (after rallying his crew with an appeal not to finish last) and goes on to compete in the archery contest, winning third place by cutting the cord that tethered the target dove.
