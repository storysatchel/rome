# Opimia (Vestal Virgin, d. 216 BC)

A Vestal Virgin convicted of *incestum* alongside her colleague Floronia shortly after Rome's catastrophic defeat at Cannae in 216 BC, amid widespread civic panic. She was executed by the standard method, buried alive under the earth at the Colline Gate.
