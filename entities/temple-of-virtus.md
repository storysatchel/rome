# Temple of Virtus

The Temple of Virtus in [[entities/rome]] was built by consul [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus]] in 208 BC, adjoining the existing [[entities/temple-of-honos]], after the pontifical college ruled he could not simply rededicate Fabius Maximus's older temple jointly to both gods. It fulfilled Marcellus's battlefield vow made over a decade earlier at [[entities/clastidium]].
