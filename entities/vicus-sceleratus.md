# Vicus Sceleratus (formerly Vicus Cyprius)

A street in [[entities/rome]], originally called the Vicus Cyprius. According to Livy, [[entities/tullia-minor]] drove her *carpentum* over the body of her murdered father, King [[entities/servius-tullius]], at the top of this street, after which it was renamed the Vicus Sceleratus ("Wicked Street") in perpetual memory of the deed.
