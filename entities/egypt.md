# Egypt (Ptolemaic/Roman)

Egypt was the Nile kingdom ruled by the Ptolemaic dynasty until its annexation as a Roman province in 30 BC. Its capital, [[entities/alexandria]], served as the administrative hub through which Red Sea and Indian Ocean trade — including the voyages of [[entities/eudoxus-of-cyzicus]] — was taxed and controlled; under Roman rule it was later visited by the emperor [[entities/hadrian]], who toured the "singing" Colossus of Memnon at [[entities/thebes-egypt]].
