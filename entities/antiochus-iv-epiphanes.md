# Antiochus IV Epiphanes

King of the Seleucid Empire, son of Antiochus III. In 168 BC he marched a large army toward Egypt to attack the Ptolemies, only to be confronted and turned back by the unarmed Roman senator Gaius Popillius Laenas. Humiliated, Antiochus returned home and took his anger out on the Jews of Judea, provoking the Maccabean revolt.
