# Gaius Cassius Longinus (consul 73 BC)

C. Cassius Longinus was consul in 73 BC alongside [[entities/terentius-varro-lucullus]]. The pair co-signed a letter to the town of Oropus confirming the senate's decision that the publicani could not assess tax on land Sulla had previously granted as inviolable to the temple of Amphiaraus.
