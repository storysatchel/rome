# Amarantus

A ship's captain, described as Jewish, who piloted the coastal vessel on which the philosopher Synesius of Cyrene sailed from Alexandria in AD 404. His ship was undermanned and under-equipped (missing a spare anchor and stormsail, both apparently pawned or sold to cover debts), nearly leading to disaster when the ship was caught in a storm. Synesius records that Amarantus alone remained cheerful during the crisis, reportedly hoping his own death would cancel his debts to creditors.
