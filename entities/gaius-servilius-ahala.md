# Gaius Servilius Ahala

Appointed master of the horse under the dictator Cincinnatus in the mid-5th century BC, Ahala killed Spurius Maelius when Maelius refused to answer a summons on suspicion of aiming at kingship. The episode became a stock precedent cited by later Romans (including by Cicero's interlocutors in *De Amicitia*) to justify extra-legal killings of men suspected of seeking tyranny.
