## Evidence

Domitian (81–96 CE) presented himself in divine terms and was later subjected to condemnation/erasure of memory ^e969627d83eb7f63
Domitian’s innovations are analyzed through the tension between novelty and anchoring in inherited Roman traditions ^e969627d83eb7f63
A bronze equestrian statue from the Sacellum of the Augustales at Misenum is best restored as Domitian hunting a crouching lion, rather than fighting a barbarian (introduction and statue analysis, PDF pp. 1–8 / printed pp. 221–228) ^f1624f893d5124da
Domitian adapted Greek/Philhellenic and Hellenistic hunting iconography into Roman concepts of virtus, with Pompeian domestic imagery as an important contemporary context (introduction, PDF pp. 1–3 / printed pp. 221–223) ^f1624f893d5124da
