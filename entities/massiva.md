# Massiva (Numidian prince)

Son of Gulussa and grandson of Masinissa, Massiva fled to Rome as a refugee after Jugurtha seized full control of Numidia. Living quietly in the city, he was persuaded by the consul Spurius Albinus to petition the Senate for the Numidian throne as a rival claimant to Jugurtha — a move that got him assassinated by hired killers acting for Jugurtha before the plan could bear fruit.
