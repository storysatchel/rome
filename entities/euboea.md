# Euboea

Euboea was the long island running along the eastern coast of central Greece, separated from [[entities/boeotia]] by the Euripus strait. Its principal city, [[entities/chalcis]], whose ambassadors complained to the senate about the praetor C. Lucretius's conduct in 171 BC, lay under Roman provincial administration as part of [[entities/achaea-greece]].
