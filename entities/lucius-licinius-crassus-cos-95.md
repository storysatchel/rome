# L. Licinius Crassus (cos. 95 BC)

Consul in 95 BC and censor in 92 BC, celebrated alongside M. Antonius as one of the two greatest Roman orators before Cicero (both are central interlocutors in Cicero's *de Oratore*). Early in his career he defended the Vestal Licinia at her 114 BC incestum trial; later he argued the winning side (for M'. Curius) in the celebrated *Causa Curiana* inheritance dispute.
