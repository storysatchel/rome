# Vibia Sabina

Wife of the emperor Hadrian, who accompanied him on his tour of Egypt in AD 130. She left a surviving inscription at the Colossus of Memnon in Thebes recording that she "twice heard Memnon" during the first hour after dawn — a successful and auspicious encounter with the famous "singing" statue, in apparent contrast to her husband's experience.
