# Marcus Folius

M. Folius served as *magister equitum* (master of the horse) to the dictator [[entities/gaius-maenius-dictator]] in 314 BC. When public pressure forced Maenius to resign over his investigation of aristocrats for electoral fraud, Folius resigned alongside him.
