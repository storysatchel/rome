# Scipio's Villa at Liternum

A squared-block, fortified villa at [[entities/liternum]] with a defensive wall, towers, narrow window-slits, and a cramped bathhouse by later Roman standards. [[entities/scipio-africanus]] lived and reportedly died here after withdrawing from Rome. Seneca tours the ruin in Letter 86 as a moral exemplar of old Roman austerity, contrasting it with his own era's taste for luxury villas and bath-houses.
