# Q. Mucius Scaevola "the Pontifex" (cos. 95 BC)

Consul in 95 BC, one of Rome's most celebrated jurists, and later pontifex maximus; murdered in 82 BC during the Marian/Sullan proscriptions. In the *Causa Curiana* (94/93 BC) he represented M. Coponius, arguing for strict literal construction of a disputed will's clause — and lost to L. Licinius Crassus's argument for the testator's evident intent. He should not be confused with his cousin, the similarly named Q. Mucius Scaevola "the Augur" (cos. 117 BC).
