# P. Cornelius Sulla (cos. des. 65 BC)

Nephew of the dictator [[entities/sulla]], elected consul for 65 BC but convicted of electoral bribery (*ambitus*) under the *lex Calpurnia* before taking office, prosecuted by L. Manlius Torquatus (assisted by Torquatus's son), who assumed the consulship in his place. Cicero later successfully defended him against a separate charge of conspiratorial involvement with Catiline (*Pro Sulla*, 62 BC).
