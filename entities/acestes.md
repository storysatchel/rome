# Acestes

Son of a Trojan mother and the Sicilian river-god Crinisus, Acestes rules the part of Sicily where Aeneas's fleet takes refuge and hosts Anchises's funeral games. He shames his old champion Entellus into fighting Dares in the boxing match, and in the archery contest his arrow catches fire mid-flight — an omen Aeneas reads as marking him for special favor, awarding him the contest's top prize despite his not striking the target.
