# Boeotia

Boeotia was a region of central Greece containing [[entities/oropus]] (whose temple of Amphiaraus held Sulla-granted tax immunity), Thebes, and Haliartus. It fell under Roman provincial administration as part of [[entities/achaea-greece]] after 146 BC.
