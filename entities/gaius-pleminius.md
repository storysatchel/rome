# Gaius Pleminius

Roman legate placed in command of the captured southern Italian port of Locri by Scipio Africanus in 205 BC. Pleminius's rapacious and violent treatment of the Locrians, including the plundering of the temple treasury of Proserpina, provoked a street brawl with two military tribunes that escalated into his own mutilation and, after Scipio departed, renewed and worsened abuses against Locri's leading citizens — ultimately triggering a Senate investigation that became a proxy battle over Scipio's fitness to invade Africa.
