# P. Cornelius Scipio Nasica Corculum (cos. 162, 155 BC)

Consul in 162 BC (and again in 155 BC) and later pontifex maximus. He was forced to abdicate his first consulship within the year after Ti. Sempronius Gracchus, the consul who had presided over the election, belatedly reported to the College of Augurs that he had committed a ritual error in conducting it.
