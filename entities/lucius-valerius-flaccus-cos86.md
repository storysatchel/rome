# Lucius Valerius Flaccus (cos. 86 BC)

L. Valerius Flaccus was murdered in 86 BC after dismissing his quaestor C. Flavius Fimbria, whom he had accused of criminality and undermining his authority. Scholarly opinion splits on whether Flaccus's reputation for tough discipline and weak leadership caused the mutiny that killed him, or whether Fimbria actively undermined and incited it.
