# Italy

The Italian peninsula, home territory of the Roman Republic. Unlike the overseas territories Rome acquired through conquest, Italy south of the Po was never organized as a Roman "province" (*provincia*) during the Republican period — it remained a patchwork of Roman citizen territory, Latin colonies, and allied (*socii*) communities bound to Rome by individual treaties, only unified administratively much later. ^[inferred] This wiki uses "Italy" as the top-level geographic node for scenarios and entities located in peninsular Italy, with sub-regions (Latium, Etruria, Campania, Samnium, Umbria, the Sabine country, Apulia, Bruttium, and Cisalpine Gaul) nested beneath it.
