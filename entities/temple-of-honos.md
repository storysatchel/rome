# Temple of Honos

The Temple of Honos in [[entities/rome]] was dedicated decades earlier by [[entities/clan-fabius|Fabius Maximus]]. In 208 BC, consul [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus]] sought to rededicate the existing structure jointly to Honos and Virtus rather than build anew, but the board of pontiffs — including Fabius himself — ruled a single temple *cella* could not be dedicated to two gods, forcing Marcellus to build the separate adjoining [[entities/temple-of-virtus]] instead.
