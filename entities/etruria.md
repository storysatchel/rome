# Etruria

Etruria was the region of west-central Italy immediately north of Latium, home to the Etruscan civilization. Early Rome — including its monarchy and several religious institutions — drew heavily on Etruscan influence before Rome's eventual conquest of the individual Etruscan cities (including [[entities/volaterrae]]).
