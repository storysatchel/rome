# C. Appuleius Decianus

Tribune of the plebs in 99 or 98 BC. He prosecuted the former tribune P. Furius for misconduct in office, a trial that ended when a mob killed Furius before a verdict. Decianus himself was later convicted on an unrelated charge and exiled to Pontus.
