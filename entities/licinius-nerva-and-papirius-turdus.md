# A. Licinius Nerva and C. Papirius Turdus

A. Licinius Nerva and C. Papirius Turdus, tribunes of the plebs in 177 BC, used public *contiones* and a proposed bill to try to strip [[entities/aulus-manlius-vulso]] of his prorogued command in Histria as the first step toward prosecuting him for his earlier military defeat. The bill was vetoed by one of their own tribunician colleagues.
