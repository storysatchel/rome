# Decius (Campanian Garrison Commander)

Polybius names Decius, a Campanian, as leader of the Roman garrison stationed at Rhegium at the end of the Pyrrhic War, which seized control of the city. Valerius Maximus gives a different account of the garrison's leadership. The garrison's fate — besieged by Rome and its survivors executed — became the subject of a tribune's complaint in 270 BC that citizen rights had been violated in the executions. ^[ambiguous]
