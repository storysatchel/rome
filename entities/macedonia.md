# Macedonia (Roman Province)

Macedonia became a Roman province after the Third Macedonian War and the final dissolution of the Antigonid kingdom (168 BC, formalized as a province in 146 BC). It bordered Roman Greece ([[entities/achaea-greece]]) and was frequently administered alongside it. As governor there (80–77 BC), [[entities/gnaeus-cornelius-dolabella-cos-81]] was accused of extorting its Greek cities, the charge behind [[entities/julius-caesar]]'s first courtroom prosecution.
