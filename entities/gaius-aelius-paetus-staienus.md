# G. Aelius Paetus Staienus (q. 77 BC)

Quaestor in 77 BC, later notorious as the juror in the 74 BC *iudicium Iunianum* accused by Cicero of accepting bribes from both the prosecution and the defense and voting to convict Oppianicus regardless. Between 74 and 70 BC he was separately prosecuted and convicted under the *lex Cornelia de maiestate* for mutinying against his commanding officer, Mam. Aemilius Lepidus.
