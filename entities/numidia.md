# Numidia

Numidia was an independent North African kingdom bordering the Roman province of [[entities/africa-province]], ruled during the late 2nd century BC by [[entities/jugurtha]]. The Jugurthine War (112-106 BC), fought here, launched [[entities/marius]]'s career at the expense of [[entities/quintus-caecilius-metellus-numidicus]], whose command Marius maneuvered away from him. Numidia did not become formal Roman territory until after Caesar's African campaign (46 BC), well after most of the events in this wiki. The town of [[entities/vaga]] within Numidia was the site of a 109 BC garrison massacre.
