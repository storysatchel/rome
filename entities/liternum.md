# Liternum

A coastal town in [[entities/campania]] where [[entities/scipio-africanus]] withdrew after deciding his continued presence in Rome threatened the Republic's constitutional balance. Seneca visits the ruined villa there in Letter 86, treating it almost as a shrine.
