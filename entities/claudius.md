# Claudius

Roman emperor from AD 41 to 54, who conquered Britain in AD 43-44. His reign is characterized in the ancient sources, especially Tacitus, as dominated by the ambitions of his successive wives Messalina and Agrippina the Younger, both of whom received unusual public honors involving the ceremonial carpentum carriage.
