# L. Manlius Torquatus (cos. 65 BC)

Consul of 65 BC. Like his colleague L. Aurelius Cotta, he won his consulship through the courts: having lost the 66 BC election to P. Cornelius Sulla, he prosecuted Sulla for electoral bribery (assisted by his own son, also named L. Manlius Torquatus, as junior prosecutor), secured a conviction, and took the consulship in Sulla's place.
