# L. Cantilius (pontifical scribe)

A scribe attached to the pontifical college (one of the "lesser pontifices"), identified in 216 BC as the lover of the condemned Vestal Floronia in the aftermath of the Roman defeat at Cannae. He was punished not by the ordinary courts but personally and directly by the Pontifex Maximus, who beat him with rods in the Comitium until he died.
