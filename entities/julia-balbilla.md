# Julia Balbilla

A poetess who accompanied the emperor Hadrian's entourage during his AD 130 visit to the Colossus of Memnon in Egypt. Her surviving verse graffiti, composed in archaic Homeric style, obliquely records that the statue failed to perform for Hadrian himself, in apparent contrast to its successful "speaking" for his wife Sabina.
