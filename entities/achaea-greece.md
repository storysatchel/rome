# Achaea (Roman Greece)

Mainland Greece came under direct Roman provincial administration as Achaea following [[entities/lucius-mummius]]'s sack of Corinth in 146 BC, the same year Macedonia and Africa were likewise formalized as provinces. It contained the historic region of Boeotia and numerous individual city-states (Athens, Thebes, Oropus) that appear as their own District-tier entities in this wiki's scenarios.
