# Licinia (Vestal Virgin, acquitted then condemned, 114–113 BC)

A Vestal Virgin tried twice for incestum: acquitted by the pontifices in December 114 BC (defended by a young L. Licinius Crassus), then retried and convicted in 113 BC by the special quaestio established under L. Cassius Longinus Ravilla. Not to be confused with the Licinia accused decades later (73 BC) alongside Fabia and M. Licinius Crassus.
