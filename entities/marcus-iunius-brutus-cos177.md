# Marcus Iunius Brutus (consul 177 BC)

M. Iunius Brutus, consul of 177 BC, joined his army with that of the previous year's defeated commander [[entities/aulus-manlius-vulso]] to finish subduing the Histri. He later returned to Rome to preside over the consular elections while tribunes campaigned to strip Manlius of his command.
