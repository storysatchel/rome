# Publius Clodius Pulcher

A young Roman aristocrat of the patrician Claudii, Clodius was notorious for his wildness and for the 62 BC Bona Dea scandal, in which he was caught disguised as a woman at a women-only religious rite. He had himself adopted into a plebeian family to qualify for the tribunate, which he used to drive Cicero into exile, and later waged a years-long gang war in the streets of Rome against his rival Titus Annius Milo, who finally had him killed on the Appian Way in 52 BC.
