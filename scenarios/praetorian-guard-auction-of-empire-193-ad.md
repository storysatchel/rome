# Praetorian Guard Auction of Empire (193 AD)

> [!tldr] A focused scenario for testing how military privilege can become imperial succession power.

## Scope

Testing how military privilege can become imperial succession power

## Scenario Evidence

Praetorian bodyguards developed from ad hoc escorts of late-Republican generals (contents, ch. 1 “Evolution,” 44–31 BC; EPUB lines 720–744) ^3171593dd4384881
Augustus converted these forces into a permanent institution after Actium, integrating personal military power into the imperial army (introduction and ch. 1, EPUB lines 724–728) ^3171593dd4384881
The Guard’s permanent concentration in Rome from AD 23 made it a direct political force; the Castra Praetoria enabled rapid intervention in the capital (introduction, EPUB lines 678–686) ^3171593dd4384881

## Decision Boundary
