# Jugurtha of Numidia

Grandson of Masinissa and heir to a share of the Numidian throne, Jugurtha used bribery of Roman senators and commissioners to expand his power, murdering his co-rulers Hiempsal and Adherbal. Summoned to Rome as a suppliant, he had his agent Bomilcar assassinate his cousin and rival claimant Massiva within the city itself, a crime that nearly caused Jugurtha's own retinue to be prosecuted. His eventual defeat in the Jugurthine War (112–106 BC) cemented Marius's rise to power.
