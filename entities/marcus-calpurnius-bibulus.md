# M. Calpurnius Bibulus (cos. 59 BC)

Consul in 59 BC alongside [[entities/julius-caesar]], and a prominent conservative opponent of Caesar's legislative agenda. Outmaneuvered politically and physically, he retreated to his house for most of the year and announced he was religiously "watching the sky" (*servare de caelo*) for unfavorable omens — a declaration which, if formally valid, should have voided any assembly held while in effect, including those that passed Caesar's laws. Whether his sky-watching carried binding legal force without a formal, in-person announcement (*obnuntiatio*) became a lasting point of dispute.
