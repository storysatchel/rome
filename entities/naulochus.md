# Naulochus

Naulochus was the site, off the coast of [[entities/sicilia]], of Octavian's decisive naval victory over Sextus Pompey in 36 BC. In the campaign's aftermath, [[entities/marcus-aemilius-lepidus]] attempted to claim Sicily for himself and challenge [[entities/augustus]]'s authority, a bid that collapsed when his own troops deserted him.
