# Titus Turpilius Silanus

Commandant of the Roman garrison at the Numidian trading town of Vaga, Turpilius was the sole officer to survive a surprise massacre of his men by the town's inhabitants during a festival in 109 BC. Bound to the general Metellus by ties of hereditary friendship, Turpilius was nonetheless court-martialed, scourged, and beheaded after an inquiry — some said at the urging of Marius — found his survival suspicious.
