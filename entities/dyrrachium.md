# Dyrrachium

Dyrrachium was a coastal town in [[entities/illyria]], a common destination for Romans sentenced to exile. [[entities/lucius-opimius]], convicted by the Mamilian Commission in 109 BC for colluding with Jugurtha, was exiled there and died.
