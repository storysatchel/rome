# Publius Decius Mus

Roman consul traditionally credited with performing the ritual of *devotio* — consecrating himself and the enemy army to the gods of the underworld before charging to his death — at the decisive Battle of Sentinum (295 BC), which broke Samnite resistance in the Third Samnite War.
