# L. Opimius (cos. 121 BC)

Consul in 121 BC, best known for suppressing C. Gracchus and his followers under the Senate's *senatus consultum ultimum* that year; acquitted in 120 BC of unlawfully killing citizens without trial in that episode. Nine years later, in 109 BC, he was convicted by the special Mamilian Commission for having taken bribes from King Jugurtha, and died in exile at Dyrrachium.
