# L. Aurelius Cotta (cos. 65 BC)

Consul of 65 BC and later censor (64 BC). He won his consulship, in substance, through the courts: having lost the original 66 BC election to P. Autronius Paetus, he prosecuted Paetus for electoral bribery, secured his conviction, and assumed the consulship himself.
