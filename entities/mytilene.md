# Mytilene

Mytilene, the chief city of the island of Lesbos within the Roman province of [[entities/asia-province]], is where [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus-cos-51-bce]] withdrew into voluntary exile after the civil war, as recorded in [[entities/marcus-junius-brutus]]'s lost treatise *On Virtue*. [[entities/julius-caesar]] deliberately avoided landing there while sailing nearby, unwilling to see Marcellus diminished.
