# Cyrene

Cyrene was a Greek city in [[entities/cyrenaica]], home of the philosopher and future bishop [[entities/synesius-of-cyrene]]. In AD 404 Synesius sailed from [[entities/alexandria]] toward Cyrene on a small, poorly equipped coastal vessel captained by the debt-ridden [[entities/amarantus]], nearly dying in a storm en route.
