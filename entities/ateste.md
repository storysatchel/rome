# Ateste

Ateste (modern Este) was a town in [[entities/venetia]]. In 134 BC it disputed boundaries with the neighboring town of [[entities/vicetia]], a dispute Rome arbitrated in the same manner as the *sententia Minuciorum* between [[entities/genua]] and the Langenses Viturii.
