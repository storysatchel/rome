# Capita Bubula

> [!tldr] A bounded account of Capita Bubula in Roman history.

## Scope

The bounded meaning and operation of Capita Bubula in the sampled Roman sources

## Core Evidence

Capita Bubula likely denotes a geometric surveying/topographic pattern rather than literal ox heads; Suetonius’ toponym locates Augustus’ birthplace near the Curiae Veteres on the Palatine; Livia converted Augustus’ birth house into a sacrarium after his death; Augustan reorganization into fourteen regions and 265 vici made him a “second founder”; the birthplace/shrine became an ideological urban centre; evidence from agrimensores is valuable but textually/dating uncertain ^7ff58e85e553ba95
