# Marcus Claudius Marcellus (consul 51 BCE)

A Pompeian-aligned consul who withdrew into voluntary exile at Mytilene after the civil war turned against his faction. He devoted himself to philosophical study; Brutus praised his life there as happier than his former consulship, in an account preserved by Seneca. He is distinct from the earlier Republican general [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus]], conqueror of Syracuse (211 BC).
