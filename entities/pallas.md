# Pallas

Son of the Arcadian king Evander, sent by his father to fight alongside Aeneas. In Book X, Turnus clears the field to face him alone and kills him despite Pallas's defiant first strike; Turnus then strips Pallas's ornate swordbelt as a trophy. Recognizing that belt on Turnus's shoulder in Book XII is what finally drives Aeneas to kill the pleading Turnus rather than spare him.
