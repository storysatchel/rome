# Bruttium

Bruttium occupied the southern "toe" of the Italian peninsula, home to Greek-founded coastal cities including [[entities/locri]] and [[entities/rhegium]]. Both cities were sites of notorious Roman military misconduct in the later 3rd century BC: Locri under the brutal occupation of legate Gaius Pleminius, and Rhegium under the mutinous garrison later known as the Legio Campana.
