# Umbria

Umbria was a region of central Italy east of Etruria, home to [[entities/interamna]] (modern Terni) — whose two-century dispute with neighboring Sabine [[entities/reate]] over the drainage of Lake Velinus was eventually argued before a Roman senatorial commission.
