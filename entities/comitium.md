# Comitium

The Comitium was an open assembly space at the edge of the [[entities/forum-romanum]], traditionally used for popular and senatorial meetings. In 216 BC the Pontifex Maximus personally flogged [[entities/lucius-cantilius-pontifical-scribe]] to death there as punishment for his affair with the Vestal [[entities/floronia-vestal-216-bc]].
