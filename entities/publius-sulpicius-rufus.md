# Publius Sulpicius Rufus

Tribune of the plebs in 88 BC, Sulpicius had been on terms of close friendship with the consul Quintus Pompeius Rufus. Championing the Marian faction's push to distribute newly enfranchised Italian citizens evenly across the voting tribes, Sulpicius turned to armed force against his former friend, triggering street violence in which Pompeius Rufus's son was killed.
