# Caracalla

Son of Septimius Severus and his co-emperor from AD 198, later sole emperor from 211 to 217. As co-emperor under his father, he was party to a joint legal rescript of AD 204 ruling against a group of Jewish merchants in a dispute over stolen goods, enforcing strict Roman property law over the merchants' appeal to Jewish legal custom.
