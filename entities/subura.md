# Subura

The Subura was a densely populated valley district of Rome, lying between the Esquiline and Viminal hills, long proverbial in Roman sources for its noise, crowding, and lower social standing relative to hills like the [[entities/palatine-hill|Palatine]]. It appears in this wiki as the setting of record for any scenario whose sources place ordinary, non-elite urban life away from the Forum or the great houses of the hills.
