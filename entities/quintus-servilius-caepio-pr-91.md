# Q. Servilius Caepio (pr. 91? BC)

Son of the Q. Servilius Caepio condemned after the 105 BC disaster at Arausio. Probable praetor of 91 BC, he prosecuted M. Aemilius Scaurus for extortion in 92/91 BC and was in turn prosecuted by Scaurus in a tit-for-tat feud involving procedural maneuvering over trial scheduling. He died fighting in the early Social War soon afterward.
