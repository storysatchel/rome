# Curia Hostilia (Senate House)

The Curia Hostilia, Rome's Senate House, stood in the [[entities/forum-romanum]]. In 100 BC, armed supporters of [[entities/lucius-appuleius-saturninus]] attempted to physically eject [[entities/quintus-caecilius-metellus-numidicus]] from the building for refusing to swear a loyalty oath. Later that same year, after the Senate declared Saturninus and [[entities/gaius-servilius-glaucia]] public enemies following the [[scenarios/the-murder-of-gaius-memmius|murder of Gaius Memmius]], Saturninus was stoned to death inside the Curia Hostilia despite assurances of safety. In 52 BC, following [[entities/clodius-pulcher]]'s death, an enraged mob burned his body inside the building, destroying it.
