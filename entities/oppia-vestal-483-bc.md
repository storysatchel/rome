# Oppia (Vestal Virgin, d. 483 BC)

The Vestal in the earliest recorded Republican-era incestum case, in 483 BC (her name is given variously in the sources as Oppia, Opimia, Pompilia, or Popillia; Livy's "Oppia" is generally preferred). Suspected after unspecified ominous portents, she was tried, convicted, and executed. If plebeian, as her probable name suggests, her case coincided with early patrician-plebeian conflict over land reform.
