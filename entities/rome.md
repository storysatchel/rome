# Rome (the city)

Rome, sited on hills along the Tiber in [[entities/latium]], was the Republic's political, religious, and ceremonial capital. Nearly every trial, senatorial debate, popular assembly, triumph, and street confrontation collected in this wiki's `scenarios/` took place somewhere within the city — most commonly in its central public spaces. This entity serves as the District-tier anchor for Rome's own internal sub-districts (hills and public areas) and, through them, the individual buildings within each.

## Sub-Districts

- [[entities/forum-romanum]] — the central civic and judicial space; site of most trials, speeches, and senatorial business covered in this wiki.
- [[entities/capitoline-hill]] — site of the Temple of Jupiter Optimus Maximus and the terminus of triumphal processions.
- [[entities/palatine-hill]] — Rome's most prestigious residential hill.
- [[entities/aventine-hill]] — historically associated with the plebeian cause.
- [[entities/campus-martius]] — the field where the centuriate assembly met and military musters were held.
- [[entities/subura]] — a crowded, lower-status residential/commercial district.
- [[entities/circus-maximus]] — the city's chariot-racing venue, in the valley between the Palatine and Aventine.
- [[entities/janiculum]] — a hill across the Tiber where a flag signaled meetings of the centuriate assembly.
