# Vaga

Vaga was a prosperous trading town in [[entities/numidia]], garrisoned by Roman troops under [[entities/quintus-caecilius-metellus-numidicus]] during the Jugurthine War. In 109 BC, incited by [[entities/jugurtha]], its inhabitants massacred the Roman garrison during a festival; [[entities/titus-turpilius-silanus]] alone escaped. Metellus retook and sacked the town days later.
