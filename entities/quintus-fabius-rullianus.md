# Quintus Fabius Maximus Rullianus

A leading member of the Fabii during the Samnite Wars, Rullianus served as master of the horse under the dictator Papirius Cursor in 325 BC, winning a battle against orders and nearly being executed for insubordination before an appeal to the Roman people saved him. He later held multiple consulships and the censorship (where he curtailed measures of his rival Appius Claudius), served as his son Fabius Gurges's legate in the Samnite Wars, and as princeps senatus opposed Scipio Africanus's invasion of Africa during the Pleminius affair.
