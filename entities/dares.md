# Dares

A powerful young Trojan boxer who had already killed the giant Butes in a Trojan-War-era bout; at the Book V funeral games he stands unopposed until the aged Entellus is shamed into fighting him. Dares is battered unconscious and loses teeth before Aeneas halts the fight to save his life.
