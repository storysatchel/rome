# Ptolemy VIII

Ptolemaic king of Egypt, nicknamed "the Pot-Bellied" by ancient sources, who funded the navigator Eudoxus of Cyzicus's pioneering voyages to India around 120 BC. His customs officials confiscated Eudoxus's spice cargo on both return voyages, prompting Eudoxus to attempt to bypass Ptolemaic control by circumnavigating Africa.
