# Gaius Laelius

Known as "Laelius the Wise," he was Scipio Aemilianus's inseparable friend and the principal speaker in Cicero's dialogue *De Amicitia*. When Laelius sought the consulship, Scipio secured a promise from Quintus Pompeius not to run against him; Pompeius broke his word, stood anyway, and won, costing Laelius the office and Pompeius his friendship with Scipio.
