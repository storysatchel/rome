# Pisae

Pisae was a coastal town in [[entities/etruria]] (modern Pisa). In 168 BC it was party to a Roman-arbitrated boundary dispute with the neighboring colony of [[entities/luna]]. This arbitration, alongside the *sententia Minuciorum* between [[entities/genua]] and the Langenses Viturii, illustrates a recurring pattern of Roman mediation in Italian land disputes.
