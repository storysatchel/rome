# Cisalpine Gaul

Cisalpine Gaul was the plain of northern Italy between the Alps and the Apennines, settled by Gallic peoples including the Insubres. In 222 BC the consul [[entities/marcus-claudius-marcellus]] campaigned there against the Insubrian king [[entities/viridomarus]], culminating in the duel at [[entities/clastidium]]. It was also where, according to Cato's censorial speech, [[entities/lucius-quinctius-flamininus]] had a man executed at a banquet to please a companion — the conduct that got him expelled from the Senate in 184 BC.
