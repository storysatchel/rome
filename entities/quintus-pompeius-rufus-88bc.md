# Quintus Pompeius Rufus (cos. 88 BC)

Consul of 88 BC alongside Sulla, Pompeius Rufus had once been in the closest and most affectionate friendship with the tribune Publius Sulpicius Rufus. That friendship collapsed into open political hatred over Sulpicius's push to enfranchise new Italian citizens across all voting tribes; the resulting street violence cost Pompeius Rufus a son. Not to be confused with Q. Pompeius, consul of 141 BC, who betrayed Scipio Aemilianus and Laelius decades earlier.
