# Lucius Caecilius Metellus

A Roman general cited in Seneca's *On the Shortness of Life* as "the only Roman to have 120 captive elephants walk in procession before his chariot" during his triumph over Carthaginian forces defeated in Sicily ^[inferred — likely the First Punic War victory at Panormus, 251 BC; Seneca's text gives no date]. Seneca uses the fact only as an example of useless antiquarian trivia, not to praise the triumph itself.
