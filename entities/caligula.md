# Caligula

Roman emperor (Gaius Julius Caesar Augustus Germanicus, r. 37-41 CE ^[inferred — dates not given in Seneca's text]). Seneca's *On Tranquillity of Mind* calls him "that second Phalaris" (after the proverbially cruel tyrant of Acragas) and presents his order for the execution of Julius Canus, following a personal altercation, as emblematic of his arbitrary cruelty — Canus is called "a grievous segment of Caligula's carnage."
