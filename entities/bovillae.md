# Bovillae

Bovillae was one of the old Latin League towns in [[entities/latium]], sited on the [[entities/via-appia]] south of Rome. It was here, near Bovillae, that the armed retinues of [[entities/clodius-pulcher]] and [[entities/titus-annius-milo]] clashed by chance on 20 January 52 BC, leaving Clodius dead and setting off rioting in Rome.
