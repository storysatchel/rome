# Cyrenaica

Cyrenaica was a Roman province on the North African coast between [[entities/egypt]] and the rest of Africa, home to the city of [[entities/cyrene]] and its native son [[entities/synesius-of-cyrene]], who sailed the Egyptian and Libyan coast between Alexandria and Cyrene in AD 404.
