# Gaius Cosconius

C. Cosconius commanded Roman forces in Illyria between 78 and 76 BC. One of his officers, M. Atilius Bulbus, incited a mutiny in a legion under his command; Cosconius restored order, and Bulbus was later tried in Rome.
