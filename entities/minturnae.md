# Minturnae

Minturnae was a small town in [[entities/latium]]. It is attested in this wiki as the home of [[entities/fannia-of-minturnae]] and [[entities/gaius-titinius-of-minturnae]], whose divorce and dowry dispute was judged by the juror [[entities/marius]] around 100 BC.
