# Manius Curius Dentatus

Manius Curius Dentatus, Roman consul in 272 BCE, is credited with cutting the first artificial canal draining Lake Velinus through the mountains into the river Nar, reclaiming the fertile Rosea plain above Reate for pasturage. The canal's downstream effects on Interamna generated a dispute that persisted for roughly two centuries, eventually reaching a senatorial commission in 54 BCE.
