# Caudine Forks (Furculae Caudinae)

The Caudine Forks was a narrow valley accessible only through two easily blocked passes, near [[entities/caudium]] in [[entities/samnium]]. There, in 321 BC, [[entities/gaius-pontius]] sealed both exits and trapped a Roman consular army, which surrendered and was marched "under the yoke" — a ritual of submission remembered as one of Rome's greatest military humiliations.
