# L. Cassius Longinus Ravilla

Consul in 127 BC and censor in 125 BC. He presided (as *quaesitor*) over the special *quaestio extraordinaria* established in 113 BC to retry the Vestals Licinia and Marcia, who had just been acquitted by the pontifical college — and convicted both of them. His reputation for severity as a judge made his name proverbial in later Roman legal culture for a harsh juror ("*Cassiani iudices*").
