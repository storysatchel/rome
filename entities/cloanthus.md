# Cloanthus

Trojan captain of the Scylla, identified by Virgil as the legendary forebear of the Roman Cluentius family. Trailing in the final stretch of the Book V boat race, he vows a sacrificial bull and wine to Neptune's company of sea deities; Portunus answers by driving his ship to the harbor first, winning him the race.
