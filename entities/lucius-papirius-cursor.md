# Lucius Papirius Cursor

Consul in 326 BC and dictator in 325 BC during the Second Samnite War, renowned (and feared) for strict military discipline. When his master of the horse, Quintus Fabius Rullianus, won a battle against explicit orders to avoid engagement, Papirius sought to execute him; only an appeal to the Roman people spared Rullianus's life.
