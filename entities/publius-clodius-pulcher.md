# P. Clodius Pulcher (aed. cur. 56 BC)

Later tribune of the plebs (58 BC) and one of the Late Republic's most notorious political operators, famous for his long feud with Cicero. Early in his public career, in 65 BC, he prosecuted L. Sergius Catilina for extortion in Africa — a prosecution Cicero himself later suggested had been conducted with suspicious "goodwill" toward the defendant, implying possible collusion (*praevaricatio*).
